Live alerts

The cards in the corner. How long each one lasts, which one never leaves on its own, and how to hold them while you read.

how-to

While you work, what is happening appears as cards in the bottom right corner. You do not have to do anything with them: they are there so you find out without leaving what you are doing.

How long each card lasts#

They do not all last the same, and the difference is deliberate.

What it isExamplesHow long
The day moving alongDriver started · arrived at the door · patient on board · trip completed4 seconds
Something went wrongCancellations10 seconds
Somebody has to actWill Call activatedNever closes on its own

Why a Will Call does not close itself

A card that treats "trip completed" the same as "a patient is waiting with the clock running" teaches you to ignore the corner of the screen. And on the day the one that mattered appears, you do not look at that one either.

So a Will Call does not behave like the rest: it comes up in its own window, sits above any other program — even when you are not in Raphael — and stays there until somebody attends it.

Holding a card that is about to go#

Put the mouse over it. The countdown stops while the pointer is on the card, and when you move away it gives you 3 more seconds before closing.

That is what to do when something appears and you cannot read the trip number in time.

When several arrive at once#

  • At most 3 cards are shown. The rest are counted in a line that says "+N more".
  • One click on that line opens the Notification Centre with everything.
  • Two notices about the same trip fold into one card, so a fast-moving trip does not fill the corner by itself.

What you will not see#

If the Notification Centre is already in front, routine notices do not pop up as cards — you are already looking at them in the list. Cancellations and Will Calls still come up.

What can go wrong#

A card closed before you read it. Nothing is lost: it is in the bell and in the Centre. The cards are a preview, never the only place a notice lives.

No cards at all. Check first whether alerts are silenced — see Alert preferences. If they are not, go to When nothing arrives.

A card is covering something. Close it with "Close". Closing it does not mark it read and does not attend it.
