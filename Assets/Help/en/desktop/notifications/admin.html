Notification administration

See whether the channel is alive, switch a notice off without deploying, review what is kept, and find out who touched what.

how-to

The administration panel answers four questions nobody else can answer: whether the channel is alive, which notices are switched on, what is being kept, and who touched what.

Administrators only. Open it with "Notification centre administration" from the Notification Centre, and come back with "Back to the inbox".

The channel — "is anything arriving?"#

The diagnostics line says three things: the hub status, when the last notification arrived, and how many are in the inbox.

A connected hub delivering nothing looks exactly like a quiet morning

This is the fault that costs the most time, because the system reports perfect health while delivering nothing.

If the panel has been empty for hours and the status says connected, it does not mean nothing happened: check the driver role configuration on the server. While that list is empty, every internal user is treated as a driver and nobody joins the office broadcast. Desktop stays empty forever, and in perfect health.

The rules — switching a notice off without deploying#

The "Notices switched on" section lists the events with their audiences, and each one is switched on and off from there.

Switching a notice off silences it in every application at once, with no deployment and with immediate effect.

A switch-off does not survive a catalog synchronisation

Synchronisation imposes what the catalog says, including what is active: it will switch back on whatever the catalog declares as on.

If the switch-off has to last, it has to be in the catalog too. Otherwise it is temporary, and nobody will tell you when it was undone.

Kept records — the list with no ceiling#

"Kept records" is the only thing the cleanup never deletes, which makes it the only list in the system without a ceiling. It needs looking at now and then.

  • "Load kept records" brings it up.
  • "Delete this record for good" deletes one.
  • "Delete every kept record" deletes them all.

Both of these deletions are permanent

They delete the row, its recipients and its data. And they undo somebody's decision to preserve it. There is no way back, and it is written down against the name of whoever did it.

The total is not the sum of the groups. One cancellation reaches the patient, the office and the driver: it appears under each of them while being one single row.

Who did what#

"Who did what" is the trail of the actions somebody chose: keeping, stopping keeping, running the cleanup and deleting kept records. Each line carries when, what, who and details.

The automatic nightly pass does not appear here: there is no person behind it.

Two things about this trail, in case anybody asks:

  • It carries no patient data. Only event codes and counts.
  • It stores the username copied, not a link to the user record. Renaming somebody cannot rewrite history.

Running the cleanup now#

"Run cleanup now" does what the nightly pass does: it expires what is past due and permanently deletes what has been expired long enough. It brings nothing forward that was not going to happen anyway; the button only moves it to the present.

Use it to confirm the cleanup works, or to free space before a review.

What can go wrong#

You load the rules and none come back. The server is not returning the catalog: that is an API problem, not a panel problem.

You switched a notice off and it still arrives. Check you switched off the right audience: an event has one rule per audience, and switching off the patient's does not switch off the office's.

You deleted a kept record you needed. It does not come back. That is why the dialog says so twice before doing it.
