Exporting notices to Excel

Put what you are looking at into a spreadsheet, to review a shift or attach it to a complaint.

how-toNew in 1.3.0

"Export to Excel" saves what you are looking at in the Notification Centre into a spreadsheet.

The steps#

  1. Go to the tab you want to export and apply a search, if you need one.
  2. Press "Export to Excel".
  3. Choose where to save it. The name is proposed with the date and time.

What comes out#

What you are looking at comes out: the tab you are on and the search you have applied. Not the whole inbox, because that is not what you asked to see.

Six columns, the same as the list:

Time · Type · Notification · Trip · Deadline · State

The export is the copy that outlives the cleanup

An office notice leaves the inbox after 12 hours. If you are going to need a shift's sequence next week — a complaint that "nobody told me" — either you keep it, or you export it now.

They are different things: keeping preserves the evidence inside the system; exporting gives you a file you can attach to an email.

Patient data#

The sheet carries no names, phone numbers or addresses. The text of the notices talks about trip numbers, dates and times, not about people.

It is still a file with operational information: store it where you store the rest, not on the desktop of a shared machine.

What can go wrong#

It says there is no data to export. The tab you are on is empty, or the search returns nothing. Go back to All and clear the search box.

Notices you expected are missing. Check two things: that you are on All, and that they are not more than 12 hours old — in that case they have already left the inbox and cannot be exported either.
