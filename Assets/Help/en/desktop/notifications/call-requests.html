Driver calls — calling back with everything in front of you

The driver asks you to call; you take their request, call with their route on screen, and close it with a reason.

how-toNew in 1.10.0

Attending a driver call means you call them, with their route, their stops and their vehicle already on screen, instead of them calling you out of the blue while you search for everything and they wait.

When you need it#

The driver has pressed Call me on their phone. You find out three ways:

  • A card appears: "… asks to be called".
  • The phone icon, next to the bell, shows how many drivers are waiting.
  • In the Notification Centre, the "Calls" chip carries the same number.

Attending a call#

  1. Open the queue: click the phone icon, or the "Calls" chip in the Notification Centre — or from here: Open the Notification Centre.
  2. Pick the oldest of the waiting requests, which is the first one in the list. Or press "Take the oldest".
  3. Press "Attend". Three things happen at once:
    • the request is in your name for the whole office, so nobody else calls them;
    • the driver gets "Dispatch is on it" on their phone;
    • the "Call" tab opens with their route, their vehicle on the map, and the call card on top.
  4. Call the number on the card. "Copy number" puts it on the clipboard.
  5. If they do not pick up, press "No answer". The driver's phone shows that you called, with a button to tell you they can talk now; when they press it, you get the card "… can talk now".
  6. When you are done, press "Resolve", choose the reason under "Why is it being closed?" and press "Close request". With "Other" you have to write the reason.
  7. "Next (N waiting)" takes the next request without going back to the queue.

Why it matters

When the driver phoned in, the call landed on whoever did not have the facts in front of them: it was cut to go and look, called back, and the driver called again. All the while they were stuck at an address they could not find, with a patient due at dialysis.

That is why a request is held in the name of whoever attends it: two people calling the same driver is a missed call for the next one. And that is why requests are attended in order of arrival: whoever has waited longest goes first, even if somebody else has pressed more often.

The list#

It reads like the inbox: one row per request, grouped under "Waiting", "Being handled" and, when you unfold it, "Closed today". Each heading carries how many there are in total.

  • It goes fifty at a time, like the inbox: ‹ and › at the top right turn the page, with the range beside them, e.g. 1–50 of 73.
  • "Closed today" is the bar at the foot, with its count. Press it to unfold or fold them.
  • Hover over a waiting request and its time gives way to "Attend".

The detail, on the right, starts with the driver's card: their state, the number to call and the buttons. Below it, "The driver's day" (whether they have left the garage, how many stops they have done, the last one and the next with how late it runs), "Vehicle and position" and the "Request history".

Handing back, taking over, reopening#

  • "Release" puts it back in the queue without losing its place: it stays ahead of the ones that arrived later. Use it if you are pulled away before you can call.
  • "Take over" moves a request somebody else holds into your name. It asks you to confirm.
  • "Reopen" puts a request closed today back to waiting. It goes to the end of the queue.

The day in numbers#

Above the queue, the strip for the day: how many requests, the average wait until somebody attends, the average handling time, and the most common reason. "Export today to Excel" exports the day's list, open and closed, without the notes.

"Today" is the day where the trips are operated, not your computer's: working from another time zone, after midnight there the requests already count for the next day.

What the driver sees#

The button on their phone tells them how their request is going, without them having to call and ask:

On their phoneWhat it means
Blue, Call meNothing requested
Orange, 7 min ×2Waiting; they have asked twice
Green, On itSomebody in the office is attending
Red, Missed callYou called and they did not answer; they can press I can talk now

Their old buttons for calling the office are still in place, greyed out, telling them to use Call me.

What can go wrong#

You press "Attend" and it says somebody else has it. Somebody beat you by a second. Pick another. The list already shows who has it.

A request has shown "Held for over 10 min" for a long time. Whoever had it may have closed Raphael or left. A held request never goes back to the queue on its own: press "Take over", confirm, and call them yourself.

"This driver's route is not available in Schedule for this day". The route is outside its dates or suspended. Call anyway, with the number on the card.

"This driver has no line assigned". A request always arrives with the driver's line, wherever they asked from; this only shows when they have none. Call them with the number in the detail, and give them a line.

"No phone on file". The driver has no phone number on their record, so the office cannot call them back. Fix their record in Employees.

A request arrives and makes no sound. Its sound is that of notices somebody has to attend: it is the first switch in Sound and silence, the same one as Will Call. If you silenced alerts for 15 minutes, the card does not show either; the phone icon keeps counting.

The note may name a patient

Write the reason, never the patient's name. Even so, treat the note as sensitive: do not copy it into emails or chats. That is why it is left out of the export.
