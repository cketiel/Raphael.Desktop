The Notification Centre

The full inbox for the shift: filter by type, find a trip, see everything that happened to it, and work it in its own window.

how-toNew in 1.3.0

The Notification Centre is the full inbox: everything that happened this shift, in a list you can filter, search and sort. It is built like a mail client, and it is used the same way.

One click on the bell opens it. — or from here: Open the Notification Centre

The columns#

ColumnWhat it is
TypeThe class of notice: Will Call, Trip Cancelled, Driver Arrived…
NotificationThe text of the notice
TripThe trip number
DeadlineThe time limit, when the notice has one
TimeWhen it arrived
StatePending, Confirmed, Overdue

The tabs#

Each tab is a filter over the same list:

All · Will Call · Cancellations · Trip Started · Driver Arrived · On Board · Completed · Needs action

"Needs action" is the one you open first

The other tabs organise information. That one gathers what nobody in the office has attended yet, which is the same count the yellow mark on the bell carries.

Start your shift there and you start with what has a person waiting behind it. Start with "All" and you start with whatever happened first, which is almost never the same thing.

Finding a trip#

In the search box, type the trip number or any text from the notice. The list filters as you type, inside the tab you are on.

To search everything, go back to All first: a search inside "Cancellations" only looks at cancellations.

Everything that happened to one trip#

  1. Turn on "Group by trip".
  2. Open the trip you care about.

"Everything about this trip" appears: the complete sequence for that trip, from scheduling to completion, in order. This is the view for answering a complaint, because it tells the story instead of handing you one isolated notice.

"Back" returns to the list.

Moving between notices#

  • Double-click a row to open the notice over the list.
  • "Older" and "Newer" step through notices without going back to the list.
  • The position is shown at the top: 3 of 47.

Taking it to its own window#

"Open in its own window" detaches the Centre and leaves it as an independent window. That is what you do with two monitors: the inbox on one, Dispatch on the other.

"Put it back in a tab" returns it inside the application.

Refreshing#

The list updates by itself as soon as something arrives. "Refresh" asks the server again, and it is good for two things: confirming the channel answers, and recovering the whole shift if the application spent a while without a connection.

What can go wrong#

The list is empty and should not be. Check the channel status at the bottom. If it says connected and still nothing arrives, go to When nothing arrives.

A notice from yesterday is missing. Office notices are visible for 12 hours from when they were created. Yesterday's is no longer in the inbox; if it needed to survive, it had to be marked as kept. See Keeping and cleanup.

You search a trip number and nothing shows. Check you are on the All tab.
