When nothing arrives

How to tell a quiet morning from a dead channel, and what to check in each case before calling support.

how-toNew in 1.3.0

An empty inbox can be a quiet morning or a fault. You tell them apart by looking at the channel status, at the bottom of the Notification Centre.

Open the Centre and check the channel status

The three states, and what to do about each#

"Live" — the channel is fine#

What you see is what there is. If it really should not be empty, carry on to the inbox is empty and should not be.

"Not receiving notifications: the connection to the server is down"#

The application has lost the channel and is reconnecting on its own.

  1. Wait a minute. It reconnects without you doing anything.
  2. If it does not come back, check you have internet by opening any other screen that loads data.
  3. When it returns, press "Refresh" in the Centre: it recovers what happened while you were out.

A notice not arriving does not mean it did not happen

Notices do not change the operation. A cancelled trip is still cancelled even if the notice reached nobody, and an activated Will Call still has its deadline running.

So with the channel down, the truth is in Dispatch and Schedules, not in the inbox. As soon as the connection is back, "Refresh" brings you the whole shift.

"No notification has arrived in a while"#

The channel says it is connected, but nothing has come in for a while. If the office is quiet, that is normal. If the office is busy, it is not: it is the warning that something may be wrong while everything looks right.

The inbox is empty and should not be#

Work through this in order:

  1. Are you on the "All" tab? A search or a filter tab leaves the list nearly empty without looking like a problem.
  2. Has it been more than 12 hours? Office notices are visible for 12 hours from when they were created. Yesterday's is gone. See Keeping and cleanup.
  3. Are your alerts silenced? That only switches off the corner cards, not the inbox, but it explains why you did not notice. See Sound and silence.
  4. Is the channel "Live" and still empty after hours? This is where what you can do ends. Pass it to an administrator with the section below.

For the administrator#

An empty inbox with a connected hub is not a quiet morning: it is the fault that costs the most time, because the system reports perfect health while delivering nothing.

The first thing to check is the driver role configuration on the server. While that list is empty, every internal user is treated as a driver, nobody joins the office broadcast, and Desktop stays empty forever without raising a single error.

The detail is in Administration.

Before calling support#

Press "Copy report for support" at the foot of any page in this help. It copies the application version, the build, your role and the channel status in one go.

Pasting that into the email saves the first three questions support is going to ask you.
