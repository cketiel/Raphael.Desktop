The notice details

What you see when you open a notice: the trip, the journey, the patient, the driver, and the jump straight into Dispatch.

reference

A notice tells you something happened. The details tell you which trip it happened to, so you do not have to go looking.

Double-click any row in the Notification Centre to open it.

The sections#

SectionWhat it holds
TripInternal number, trip number, day and time window
JourneyPickup and dropoff: where, when it was scheduled, when it was reached, when it finished
Trip detailsTravel time, distance travelled, whether it is a Will Call, whether it is cancelled, no-show reason
BillingWhat relates to charging for the trip
LineThe leg: whether this is the outbound or the return

Remember that a round journey is two trips. These details describe one of them. See Leg — outbound and return.

Patient and driver#

  • "Patient contact details" expands the patient's contact information.
  • "Driver and vehicle" expands who is carrying them and in what.

Both load when you expand them, not before. If you see "Loading the patient's details…", it is asking the server for them.

The two actions that save time#

  • "Open in Dispatch" — jumps to the trip in the Dispatch tab, already selected. It is the short path between finding out about something and doing something about it.
  • "Copy trip number" — copies the number to the clipboard, to paste into an email or read out over the phone without getting a digit wrong.

When the trip no longer exists#

If the notice points at a trip that has been deleted, the details say "This trip no longer exists".

The notice is not wrong: it describes something that really happened. What is gone is the trip. That is normal for test trips and for imports that were undone; if it happens with a real, recent trip, it deserves a question.
