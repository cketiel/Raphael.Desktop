The bell and its two marks

The red number and the yellow mark count different things. Confusing them leaves a patient waiting.

referenceNew in 1.3.0

The bell sits at the top right, next to the menu, and is always visible. Two different marks can appear on it, and they do not mean the same thing.

The red number, top right#

It counts what you have not opened.

It is a personal count, and this computer's. It goes down when you open the notices. It does not go down because a colleague opened them, and it does not follow you if you sit at another machine tomorrow.

The yellow mark, bottom left#

It counts what nobody in the office has taken charge of.

It is the team's count, not yours. It only goes down when somebody — you or anyone else — confirms the notice. Hovering over it says how many are waiting.

Why there are two and not one#

Red numberYellow mark
What it countsWhat you have not seenWhat nobody has attended
Whose it isYours, and this computer'sThe whole office's
How it goes downBy opening the noticeBy confirming the notice
What is behind itInformationA person waiting

The yellow mark is the one that cannot stay

A high red number at the end of a shift means it was a busy shift. A yellow mark two hours old means there is a patient at a clinic door that nobody has sent a vehicle to.

They are two different facts, which is why the system marks them separately. If you are only going to watch one, watch the yellow one.
