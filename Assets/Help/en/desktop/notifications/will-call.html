Attending a Will Call

The patient has said they are ready and the clock is already running. What confirming means, and what you promise when you do it.

how-to

Confirming a Will Call tells the patient the dispatch office is arranging their ride, and commits to a vehicle reaching them before a specific time.

It is the one action in the Notification Centre that leaves the screen and reaches a person.

When you need it#

An alert has gone off that does not close on its own and has placed itself above everything else. There is a yellow mark on the bell. In the Centre, the "Needs action" tab has something in it.

That means a patient has finished their appointment and is waiting.

The steps#

  1. Open the notice, from the floating window or from the "Needs action" tab.
  2. Read the deadline on the card. That is the time by which a vehicle has to have arrived.
  3. Assign the trip to a route that can make it by then. Use "Open in Dispatch" to go straight to the trip, or open Dispatch from here.
  4. Come back to the notice and press "Confirm".
  5. Confirm in the dialog. The notice becomes Confirmed and the yellow mark goes down.

The deadline belongs to the patient, not to you

The time limit is counted from when the patient said they were ready, not from when you opened the screen. If the notice sat unread for forty minutes, you do not have an hour: you have twenty minutes.

And confirming is not "seen". Confirming sends the patient a message saying their ride is being arranged and a vehicle will reach them at that time. It is a promise with your office behind it, made with one button.

The three states#

StateWhat it means
PendingNobody has taken charge. The patient is waiting without knowing anyone has seen it
ConfirmedSomebody in the office took charge, and the patient knows
OverdueThe deadline passed. The patient is still waiting and nobody has delivered

Confirming belongs to the team, reading belongs to you#

When you confirm, it is confirmed for the whole office: nobody else is going to call the same patient about the same thing. Marking it read, on the other hand, only affects you.

The system does not record who confirmed

The office sees Confirmed, but not whose hand it was. If your office needs to know, keep your own note: the system will not be able to tell you afterwards.

Where Will Calls come from#

  • The patient, from their application, when they say they are ready.
  • Customer service, when the patient phones instead of using the application.

A Will Call cannot yet be activated from Desktop. If you have to set a time by hand, clear the Will Call mark on the trip and set the pickup time, from the open trips grid on the Schedules tab.

What can go wrong#

The floating window is gone. Nothing is lost: the notice is still on the bell and in the "Needs action" tab.

You confirmed and cannot make the time. Confirming cancels nothing and locks nothing. It is still an ordinary trip: reassign it in Dispatch. What cannot be undone is the message the patient already saw, so phone them if the delay is going to be a long one.

The notice is Overdue and the patient has already travelled. That happens when it was sorted out by phone without touching the notice. Confirm it anyway so the yellow mark goes down, or the office keeps seeing a pending item that is not one.

You marked a trip as Will Call by mistake. It is undone from the same grid on the Schedules tab, and it produces its own notice so the office finds out.
