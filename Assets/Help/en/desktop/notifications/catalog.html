The notices, one by one

What triggers each notice, which ones reach the office and which do not, and what you are expected to do with each.

reference

The system has ten notices and one signal. Not all of them reach the office, and knowing which ones do not saves half an hour looking for something that was never sent.

What reaches the office#

NoticeDoes the office see it?
Trip scheduledNo
Trip startedYes
Trip cancelledYes
Trip reactivatedYes
Will Call createdYes
Will Call activatedYes
Will Call confirmedNo
Driver arrived at pickupYes
Passenger on boardYes
Trip completedYes
Driver route updatedNo — it is the driver's

The ones that reach the office#

Trip started#

Triggered by the driver, when they take the trip in their application and set off for the pickup. It says a driver has started the trip and is heading to the pickup location. What to do: nothing. This is the day moving along.

Trip cancelled#

Triggered by whoever cancels: the office, the driver, the patient, the requesting facility, the requesting organization or customer service. The notice says which of the six it was. What to do: check whether the slot it frees can be reused, and whether the return leg still stands.

A driver's cancellation takes the return with it

When the driver cancels, the other legs for that patient the same day are cancelled too. When anybody else cancels, they are not. If the cancellation came from the driver, check whether the return went down as well.

Also, on the Schedules tab: if you have the open trips grid in front of you and the cancelled trip is in it, the row is marked as cancelled, the grid brings it into view, and a few seconds later it is removed. It is the only thing a notice changes on a working screen, and it is there so nobody schedules a trip that no longer exists.

Trip reactivated#

Triggered by undoing a cancellation. It says the trip is active again and that it needs a route. What to do: assign it a route. A trip coming out of cancellation does not have one.

Will Call created#

Triggered by a dispatcher, turning a trip into a Will Call from the open trips grid on the Schedules tab. It says the trip waits for the patient to say they are ready, and no vehicle is committed. What to do: nothing yet. See Will Call.

Will Call activated#

Triggered by the patient from their application, or customer service on their behalf. It says the patient is ready, and by what time a vehicle has to reach them. What to do: everything. See Attending a Will Call.

Driver arrived at pickup#

Triggered by the driver, pressing Arrive on the pickup event. What to do: nothing, unless the patient calls saying they cannot see the vehicle.

The patient also gets an SMS, sent by the driver's application on its own. Two notices about the same fact by different routes: the SMS also reaches somebody who has not installed the passenger application.

Passenger on board#

Triggered by the driver, marking the pickup as done. Only the office sees it. The patient is inside the vehicle; they do not need their phone to tell them.

Trip completed#

Triggered by the driver, marking the dropoff as done. What to do: nothing. It closes the trip's sequence.

The ones that do not reach the office, and why#

Trip scheduled#

It goes to the patient and, if the trip is theirs, to the organization that requested it. Not to the office, because whoever schedules the trip is looking at the screen where they just did it. Telling them would be repeating what they just typed.

Will Call confirmed#

It goes only to the patient: it is the message telling them the office has taken charge. Inside the office that information is already in the state of the notice, which becomes Confirmed.

Driver route updated#

It is a signal, not a notice, and it goes only to the driver's application. It tells them the schedule on their screen no longer matches reality, because somebody added or removed a trip.

Nobody has to do anything with it. It is issued by the server, never by Desktop, and only if the driver has already left the garage that day.
