One driver, one route a day

Why a driver can only have one route in service each day and every route its own smartphone login, and what to do when saving finds the driver already has one.

how-toNew in 1.15.0

A driver works one route a day, and every route in service has its own smartphone login. That way the Driver app always knows which route to open at sign-in, and no phone shows another route's patients.

Both rules look from today on. Two of a driver's routes overlapping in the past never stops you editing either.

When the notice appears#

When you save a route, new or edited, if:

  • the driver you picked already has another route in service on any of those days, or
  • the smartphone login is already used by another route in service.

What you can do#

The Route conflict dialog opens, with one card for each route in the way:

  1. Use that route instead: closes the one you were editing, without saving it, and opens the driver's route so you change it there (vehicle, hours, whatever is needed).
  2. End that route on [date]: the date is the day before yours starts. It asks you to confirm, ends it and saves yours.
  3. Back to the route: to pick another driver or type another login.

End is not available while the other route still has trips routed after that date: the dialog says how many and from which day. Move them to another route first, so no trip is left without a driver who sees it on their phone.

If the vehicle breaks down mid-day#

The driver has done the Pull-out and some trips, and the vehicle breaks down. Create a new route for that driver with the other vehicle, starting today. When you save, the dialog also offers:

  • End [the broken-down vehicle's route] today and go on with this route: the old route ends today and the new one carries on the following days. Only offered when the old one has no trips routed from tomorrow on.
  • Save this route for today only: the new route is valid today and the old one goes on tomorrow, for example when the vehicle comes back repaired.

The dialog says how many of today's stops are still pending on the old route: move them to the new one. The ones already done stay where they are, as the day's history. From then on the driver's phone opens the new route when they go back to the Dashboard, with its own Pull-out (and its inspection, if the Provider has it on). The office closes the broken-down vehicle's Pull-in.

The handover only works for today. For a change planned for another day, end the old route the day before.

The end date#

The end date can't be before the start date. Nor can you bring a route's end date forward past trips already routed on it after that day.

Why it matters

With two routes in service, the driver's phone opened either one: some days their Dashboard was empty while their patients waited on the other. And two routes sharing a login showed the same patients on two phones, and both drivers could mark them.

CHANGE ROUTE in the Driver app#

A driver can still open a colleague's route with CHANGE ROUTE, but only one of their own company. A route of another company tells them it isn't available.
