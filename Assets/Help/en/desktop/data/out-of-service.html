Take a vehicle or a driver out of service

Take a vehicle, a driver or both out of service, what happens to their trips, how to give the route another vehicle or driver, and how to put them back.

how-toNew in 1.13.0

When a vehicle breaks down or a driver cannot work, you take it out of service. From that moment its routes take no trips, and every trip they had pending, today and on later days, goes back to the unscheduled list so you can give it to another route. It stays that way until somebody puts it back.

It is not the same as closing a route with its to date (the route ends) or a suspension (a few days planned ahead). Out of service is the unexpected, with no date to come back.

When you need it#

  • The vehicle has a breakdown, an accident, an expired registration or insurance, or its ramp fails.
  • The driver is on leave, has an expired license, or did not show up.

The steps#

  1. Choose where from:
    • Data → Scheduling → Runs: the red crossed-out car button on the route's row. From here you say what is wrong: the vehicle, the driver or both.
    • Data → Scheduling → Vehicles: the same button on the vehicle's row.
    • Admin → Employees: the same button on a driver's row. At the top, three steps show where you are: 1 Reason → 2 See what it does → 3 Confirm. The current step is purple, and a box above the buttons says what is missing.
  2. For each one you take out, choose a Reason from the list. If you choose Other, write a note.
  3. Press See what it does (the highlighted button at that step). It saves nothing. Route by route and day by day, every pending trip appears with its Pickup and Dropoff, their hours, and what will happen to it:
    • goes back to the unscheduled list: the usual;
    • PATIENT ON BOARD, in red: the patient is already in the vehicle, so the trip stays on the route to be finished;
    • stays on the route with the new vehicle or driver: if you choose a substitute (step 4).
  4. If you have a spare vehicle or driver, choose it under Instead, give the route. The route keeps its trips, and the change stays on the route until somebody edits it. Then press Update the preview.
  5. Check the summary at the bottom ("N trip(s) (M events) will go back to the unscheduled list") and press Take out of service. Confirm.
  6. The result says how many trips went back, with one link per day: Open the unscheduled trips of…, with these picked out. A Schedule tab opens with those trips in amber.

It is all saved together, or not at all: if something fails, no trip is left half-done.

The red button starts off

Take out of service only turns on after See what it does, and turns off again if you change anything (reason, note, substitute) until you press Update the preview. While it is off, the pointer turns into 🚫 over it and a tooltip says what is missing. No field is missing: the preview is.

What the others see#

  • Schedule: the route is no longer in the list of routes to route on. Whoever had it open sees a red notice, "Route … is out of service", and cannot route on it. Their unscheduled list updates by itself and picks out the trips that arrived.
  • Runs, Vehicles and Employees: a red Out of Service badge in the Service column. Hover it to see since when and why. In Runs, Only out of service filters them. These three screens update by themselves, on your computer and on everyone else's, without reloading: the badge appears or goes, and Runs shows the substitute vehicle or driver.
  • Dispatch: on the map, the vehicle is grey with a red ring.
  • The office gets the Route Out of Service notification, with the cause, the reasons and how many trips went back.
  • The driver: their app reloads the route and the trips leave their list.
  • The clinic and the patient never see "out of service": they see their trip waiting for a vehicle, like any unscheduled trip.

Back in service#

Press the green Back in service button on the same row and confirm. The route takes trips again, and the office gets Back in Service. The trips that went back to the unscheduled list do not come back by themselves: route them again.

Why it matters

A broken vehicle with trips on it is a patient waiting at the door for somebody who is not coming, perhaps for dialysis. Taking all its trips off at once, and seeing which they are, is what lets you give them to another route in time. And a patient already on board cannot be left in a vehicle no screen shows: that is why that trip is not touched.

What can go wrong#

  • "The trips of these routes changed since the preview": somebody routed or took off a trip while you were reviewing. Nothing was done; the preview comes back up to date.
  • "It is already out of service": somebody got there first. Reload the screen.
  • The substitute is refused: it is out of service, already works another route on one of those days, or cannot carry one of the trips (a wheelchair trip in a vehicle with no ramp, for example). Choose another.
  • A trip had the patient on board: it stays on the route. Make sure it is finished.
  • Do not write the driver's medical details in the note. The reason is enough.
