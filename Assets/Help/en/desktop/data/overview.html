Data — the master data

Patients, locations and the tables planning needs in order to calculate anything.

referenceNew in 1.3.0

Data is where what does not change every day lives — and everything that does change depends on it.

Four sections:

SectionWhat it holds
CustomersThe patients: details, contact, and what shapes their trips
SchedulingVehicles, routes and what planning uses to calculate
LocationThe regular locations, so addresses are not typed every time
OtherThe remaining supporting tables

Wrong data here does not raise an error: it produces a badly calculated route, or a signature demanded from a driver who should not have been asked. See Space type and Funding source.
