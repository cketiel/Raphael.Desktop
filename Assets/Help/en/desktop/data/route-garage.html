Changing a route's garage

What happens to the days already planned when you change the garage address of a route, and what does not change.

how-toNew in 1.13.0

Every day of a route starts with a Pull-out (the vehicle leaves the garage) and ends with a Pull-in (it comes back). When you change the garage address in the route editor, the change also reaches the days that already have trips planned, and each of those days is recalculated.

When you need this#

The vehicle now sleeps somewhere else, or the garage address was wrong.

The steps#

  1. Open the route in its editor and change the garage address.
  2. Press save. Analysing the garage change appears first, with a progress bar, while each day is worked out. You can press Stop: nothing is saved.
  3. If the route has planned days, a dialog opens before saving with what will happen, day by day:
    • the Pull-out departure, before and after, and the drive to the first stop;
    • the Pull-in arrival, before and after, and the drive back;
    • every stop whose ETA or wait changes, with its old and new value. If none changes, it says so.
  4. Press Save and recalculate to apply it, or Cancel to go back to the editor without saving.
  5. Applying changes appears. Do not close that window: everything is saved together, or nothing is. When it ends, a message confirms the changes were applied.

If you stop Applying changes before the server had finished, nothing is saved — neither the route nor its days. If it had already finished, the change stays saved in full, and the message says so.

What changes and what does not#

  • Past days: not touched. They are the record of what happened.
  • Today, with the vehicle already out: the Pull-out does not change — it left from where it left. The Pull-in returns to the new garage, and the last leg is recalculated.
  • Today, with the vehicle already back: that day does not change.
  • Today before leaving, tomorrow and after: the Pull-out and the Pull-in change, and the whole route is recalculated: the legs are measured again and every ETA and wait is re-chained.

Usually only the Pull-out and Pull-in hours move: the vehicle leaves earlier or later by exactly as much as the first drive changes, and the rest of the day stays the same. But the whole day can move. That happens when the departure would run into midnight, when the first stop promises no hour, or when the traffic at that hour is no longer the same. The dialog shows it before you save.

Why it matters

If the route says one garage and its days another, the driver leaves from the old address, the departure is worked out for a different drive and the wait at the first stop is not real. The patient waits for a vehicle that left late.

What can go wrong#

  • "The drive between the new garage and the route could not be measured": the address or its coordinates are not valid. Nothing is saved; fix the address and try again.
  • "The route changed while you were reviewing": another dispatcher moved the route, or the day moved on, between the analysis and your confirmation. Nothing was saved, and the dialog comes back with the updated figures for you to review again.
  • "What was applied" appears after saving: between the preview and the save another dispatcher changed the route, or the traffic gave another time. The change is saved; the dialog shows what it really did.
  • The driver is already out: their app gets "route updated" and reloads the route.
