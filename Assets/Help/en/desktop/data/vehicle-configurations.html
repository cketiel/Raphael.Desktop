Vehicle configurations and classes of seat

Defining what fits in each kind of vehicle, what vehicle each class needs and who travels alone.

how-toNew in 1.23.15

A configuration says how many patients of each type fit in a vehicle. Only administrators see and change it, in Data → Scheduling → Manage vehicles → Configurations.

How what a vehicle carries is decided#

Its largest seat decides, and it can take everything below it: a stretcher makes it STR; a wheelchair, WCH; seats only, AMB.

Creating or changing a configuration#

  1. Press New configuration, or the pencil in a row.
  2. Give it a name.
  3. Add one line per space type, with how many fit. Above you see the drawing and what it will carry.
  4. Press Save.

If vehicles use it and the change gives them another kind, it tells you how many before saving.

The classes of seat#

On the right, each class says:

  • Needs a vehicle: the least that can carry it. BWCH and WCX go with WCH; BSTR goes with STR.
  • Travels alone: with that patient on board nobody else travels. Without the tick, how many ride together decides.

Change the value and press Save in its row.

Today quantities inform, they do not block

The routing suggestions use these limits. Routing by hand stays free: it only warns when the kind of vehicle does not serve.

One configuration changes many vehicles

Take the stretcher out of one that five vans use and all five stop suiting stretcher trips: the dispatchers will see the warning on every one of them.

What can go wrong#

I cannot change a row. It belongs to the common catalog: only Raphael's administrators change it.

"There is already a configuration with that name". The name exists; use another one.

How many at once#

In each configuration you choose the Patients on board at the same time (2) and Of those, how many may be in a wheelchair or on a stretcher (1). Two ambulatory patients ride together, or one with a wheelchair or a stretcher, but never a wheelchair with a stretcher. The routing suggestions use them.
