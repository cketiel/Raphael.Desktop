Raphael Desktop in five minutes

What each tab does, what each role sees, and where to start a shift.

whyNew in 1.3.0

Raphael Desktop is the office application: trips are booked here, planned here, watched here, and answered for here when something goes wrong.

The tabs#

The six main menu tabs are at the top right. They are not all always visible: it depends on your role.

TabWhat it is for
HomeBooking a trip
DataMaster data: patients, locations, and what planning needs to know
SchedulesPlanning the day and seeing it in numbers
DispatchWatching the routes that are running
ReportsThe reports
AdminEmployees, billing, integrators and providers

Next to them, always visible, is the bell: what is happening on the road. See What the system tells you.

Who sees what#

  • Administrator — every tab, including Admin.
  • Dispatcher — everything except Admin.
  • Driver — Reports only.

If you expect a tab and it is not there, it is not hidden: your role does not include it.

Where to start a shift#

  1. The bell. If it has a yellow mark, start there: something is unattended, and there is a person waiting behind it.
  2. Dispatch, to see which routes are running and how they are doing.
  3. Schedules, for what still has no vehicle.

Language and version#

  • The language is changed from the gear, top left. The application switches immediately, without restarting. This help switches with it.
  • The version you are running appears in the window title and on the way in. It is the first thing support will ask you for.
