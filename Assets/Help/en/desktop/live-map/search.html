Search: lines, trips and places

The three search boxes of the dock, what each one finds, the cities and the garages.

referenceNew in 1.23.0

SEARCH, on the dock at the bottom, has three different boxes. Choosing the right one is what makes it find.

BoxWhat it searchesWhat to typeWhat happens
1 · lineLinesLine name, driver, plate or vehicleSelects the line and frames it
2 · tripToday's tripsTrip Id (Raphael's or the broker's) or patient nameSelects the vehicle carrying it and puts TRIP … above it
3 · placePlaces on the mapCity, address, hospital… or coordinates "26.64, -81.87"Moves the map. Selects nothing

Using it#

  1. Press SEARCH, or rest the pointer on it for a moment. The tray opens.
  2. Type in the box. In 1 and 2 results show as you type, with a tag: the line's group, or the trip's state (ON BOARD, EN ROUTE).
  3. Choose with the arrows and Enter, or with a click. In 3, press the magnifier first and end with Enter.

Trips in progress come first. Esc closes the results and, again, the tray. It folds by itself when the pointer leaves; the pin keeps it open.

Cities and garages#

Next to box 3 there is a pill per city the fleet works in. Pressing it moves the map there and shows its garages, with the keys Q W E R T.

What can go wrong#

What you seeWhy
No line matchesMisspelt, or you were looking for a trip: use box 2
No trip matches todayIt is of another day: search it in Home
You choose a trip and nothing happensIt has no run, or its line is not on the map
The map does not moveThe place was not found. Type it in full, with the city
