POWER UP: keys and costs

The OpenAI, Google Maps and TomTom keys: what each one is for, how they are saved and what they cost.

referenceNew in 1.23.0

The map works with no keys. POWER UP, bottom right, adds capabilities with your keys. Its button says how many are missing, or POWERED UP.

ProviderWhat it enablesState
OpenAIVoiceAvailable
Google MapsThe Delays on each route layerAvailable
TomTomThe Delays TomTom layerTrial
Anthropic, Grok, OpenRouter, Mapbox, HERE, AmazonLaterDisabled

Saving a key#

  1. Press POWER UP.
  2. With no key, GET KEY opens the provider's page. With one saved, it says MANAGE.
  3. Paste it in its row and press SAVE KEYS. Google's and TomTom's are tested first: if the provider does not accept them they are not saved, and it says why.
  4. Esc to close.

They are saved on this computer, for your Windows user, and never shown again. To change one, paste another over it. Voice uses the new one on its next session.
