The selected line: road, Follow and Details

How a line is selected, what its road draws, what Follow is for and what each piece of the Details card says.

referenceNew in 1.23.0

Selecting a line tells the map who you want to know about. Only one at a time.

Selecting and letting go#

Select: one click on its vehicle, in DRIVERS, in the In progress list, in the SEARCH results, or by voice. It is framed and its road is drawn. Let go: a click on the empty map, the X of the bar or Esc.

The selection bar#

ElementWhat it does
Colored dot and nameThe pace, the line and its driver
Trip in progressDraws only the trip it carries now
Whole dayDraws the whole day, from Pull-out to Pull-in
FollowThe camera stays with the vehicle
DetailsOpens its card
XLets go of the selection

Below, the legend of what is being drawn.

What "Trip in progress" draws#

  • Red pin, the pickup, and blue pin, the drop-off, with their times.
  • Grey, already driven; in the pace color, still to go, with arrows for the direction.
  • Dots, the way to the pickup, if it has not arrived.
  • With everything done, the road to the garage, with the green house. With no trip started, nothing.

What "Whole day" draws#

  • The stops numbered in order: green pickup, red drop-off, purple where it has arrived. Several in one place share a chip.
  • The road already driven is animated from stop 1, in grey. The current stretch, in the pace color. The plan, dashed.
  • The green house is the garage: leaving (Pull-out) and coming back (Pull-in).
  • Stops of cancelled trips do not show. An empty stretch with no saved road is a straight line.

Follow#

It is for not losing sight of a moving vehicle, above all up close: without Follow, when you zoom in the vehicle leaves the screen. Pressing it brings the map closer and keeps the vehicle in the centre while it moves, at any zoom. Zoom with the wheel: it keeps following. If you drag the map it pauses and returns on release. It ends by pressing Follow again, with Esc or by choosing another line. Also in 3D.

The Details card#

Opened with Details or a double click on the vehicle. Closed with the X, Esc or a press outside.

PieceWhat it says
DrawingThe kind: AMB minivan, WCH van with a ramp, STR van with a stretcher. From its configuration
HeaderLine, vehicle, plate, driver, group and what fits
Bar and clockOnly with a trip in progress: where it is, minutes and miles left and the arrival time. Red when late
DriverName and phone
NowSpeed, or Stopped, and how old the position is
Trips todayDone, total and cancelled
Next stopThe stop's name as in Dispatch, with the patient, its promised time and its ETA. Before leaving, Pull-out; with everything done, Pull-in; then No stops left today
ShiftHours and miles
Charged todayThe charges of its trips not cancelled
DocumentsOf the vehicle and of the driver: expired, the next to expire, or up to date
ButtonWhat it opens
Route in DispatchAnother Dispatch tab on this route, with the trip in progress picked out
Trip in HomeHome searching the trip in progress. Only if there is one
Vehicle documents / Driver documentsTheir documents in Compliance
Copy phoneThe driver's phone, ready to paste

The next stop is worked out so: no cancelled trips; first what is ahead of the last stop done; a stop left behind undone only counts when nothing is ahead.

What can go wrong#

What you seeWhy
No stops left todayIt did the Pull-in, or has no trips
Trip in progress draws nothingThe driver has not started the next trip. Try Whole day
An old next stopA stop was not marked as done. Check it in Dispatch
Follow does not moveIt is stopped or its position is old
