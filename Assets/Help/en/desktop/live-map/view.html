The view: buttons, windows, styles and 3D

The six top buttons, the map source, the visual styles, 3D, Street View and how to move and zoom the map.

referenceNew in 1.23.0

Moving around the map#

Drag to move, mouse wheel to zoom in and out. None of this changes data.

The six top buttons#

Pointing at each one says its name and what it does.

ButtonWhat it doesHow to leave
Street ViewPress it and then click a street (in blue, those with pictures)Exit Street View or Esc
Map viewA panel to zoom and move step by stepThe same button
Full screenThe map in its window, covering the screen, with panels and dockEsc or the same button
Fit vehicles on screenAdjusts the view so every shown vehicle is seen—
Hide panelsLeaves only the mapThe same button or H
Own windowMoves the map to another window, for a second monitor for instanceThe same button brings it back to the tab

Changing window reloads the map in a few seconds and ends the voice session.

VISUAL PRESETS#

They change only the look. They open like SEARCH and have a pin too.

KeyStyleFor
1NormalWorking
2CRTOld green monitor
3NVGNight vision: comfortable in the dark
4FLIRThermal contrast
5AnimeBright colors with outlines
6NoirHigh-contrast black and white
7SnowBright and cold

It is remembered on this computer. The panels' accent color changes with it.

MAP SOURCE#

Map · No labels (the one it opens with: vehicles read better), Google Map, Satellite, Sat · No labels and 3D.

3D#

Real buildings and terrain. Vehicles move the same, are selected with a click, the chosen line's road is drawn and Fit and Follow work. Go back by choosing another map source. The steps of Map view and zoom by voice move the flat map.

What can go wrong#

What you seeWhat to do
No street imagery hereClick on a blue street, closer
The panels are goneThey are hidden: press H
The map does not fit the screenEsc to leave full screen
