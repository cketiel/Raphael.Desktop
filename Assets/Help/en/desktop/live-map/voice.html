Voice: talking to the map

How to turn on the voice assistant, what you can ask, what it does not do, what it knows and what it costs.

referenceNew in 1.23.0

MIC, in the middle of the dock, is an assistant you talk to that operates the map for you. It understands Spanish and English and answers in the language you speak. It knows every element of this screen and reads this help when you ask how something works.

Before you start#

It needs the OpenAI key in POWER UP and microphone permission. The conversation is billed to that key.

Turning it on and off#

Press MIC or the Space bar to turn it on, and again to turn it off. While on, it listens all the time: nothing has to be held. Turn it off when you finish.

StateWhat it means
OFF / VOICE STANDBYNot listening
CONNECTINGAsking for the microphone and opening the session
LISTENINGTalk
EXECUTINGDoing what it says below
ERRORThe reason is below

STD / MINI chooses the model: STD understands better, MINI is cheaper. It applies to the next session. The cost figure next to it is not measured yet: check the spending in your OpenAI account.

What you can ask#

Say, for instanceWhat it does
"Show me Aisha", "line LM-04", "plate LM03FL"Selects the line
"Where is trip 278?"Selects the vehicle carrying it
"Go to Cape Coral", "take me to the hospital on Cleveland Avenue"Moves the map
"Look for a patient"Opens the second SEARCH box for you to type
"Closer", "zoom out a lot", "move left", "show the whole fleet"Moves the view
"Follow it", "stop following"Follow
"Whole day", "only the trip"Which road is drawn
"Open the details", "open it in Dispatch", "its documents"The card and its buttons
"When does it arrive?", "what is left?", "is it late?"Answers about the line
"How is the day?", "how many are left to route?"Answers with TODAY
"Which trips are in progress?", "who is late?", "who is speeding?"Answers or opens the list
"Show me the cancelled ones", "the ones without a run"Opens Home or Dispatch in another tab
"Show the suspended ones", "remove the traffic", "the routes"Filters and layers
"Hide the panels", "full screen", "own window", "refresh"Windows
"Night vision", "satellite", "3D"Style and map source
"What does the yellow frame mean?", "open the help"Reads or opens this help

What it does not do#

  • It does not say or search patient names, phones or addresses, and Raphael does not send them to it. What you say aloud does reach OpenAI: do not say patient names.
  • It does not turn on the delays layers, which cost money: tick them yourself. It does turn them off.
  • It changes no trip: it does not route, cancel or write. For that it takes you to Dispatch or Home.
  • It does not see the screen: it knows what Raphael tells it.

What can go wrong#

What you seeWhat to do
OPENAI_API_KEY is not setSave the key in POWER UP
Microphone permission deniedAllow it in Windows, Settings, Privacy, Microphone
Connection lostPress MIC again
It does not get a lineSay the driver's name or the plate
It went off when changing windowThat is normal: turn it on again
