Live map — the fleet of the day

What the live map is, how to open it, the areas of the screen, its states, the shortcuts and what to do in each situation.

referenceNew in 1.21.2

The live map shows, on Google Maps, where every vehicle is now, how it is doing against the clock and how the day is going. It is for the office: dispatchers and administrators. It changes no trip: here you look, search and jump to Dispatch, Home or Documents to act.

Opening and closing it#

Press the globe icon on the top bar. It opens in a tab, closed with its X. When somebody else routes or changes a route, the map finds out by itself.

The words you need#

WordWhat it is
LineA run of the day: a vehicle with its driver. It is what you select on the map.
StopA patient's pickup or drop-off.
Pull-out / Pull-inLeaving the garage and coming back: the start and the end of a line's day.
Trip in progressThe driver has started it: on the way to the pickup, at the door or with the patient.
PaceWhether the line is on time: green, yellow or red.

What can go wrong#

What you seeWhat happens and what to do
Could not load the mapPress Refresh, the circular arrow of the right panel
A vehicle is missingIts group is off in FILTERS, or it has sent no position in 7 days
A faded vehicleIts last position is over 5 minutes old: no signal, phone off or app closed
The pill says Not connectedThe live channel was lost. It reconnects by itself; otherwise, Refresh
