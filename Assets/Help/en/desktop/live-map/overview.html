Live map — the day's fleet

What the live map shows, what the colours mean (green, yellow, red), how speeding is marked, the panels, the top buttons, the dock, POWER UP and the Delays on each route layer with its cost.

referenceNew in 1.21.2

The live map shows, on Google Maps, where every vehicle of the fleet is right now, how it is doing for time and what it has done today. Open it with the globe icon in the top bar.

The vehicle on the map#

Each vehicle is a disc with a van seen from above and its line's name below. The van turns with the GPS heading: its nose always points where it goes.

What you seeWhat it means
Disc colourThe line's group: on route today, active with no trips, suspended, out of service, expired (the same colours as the filters).
Corner brackets round the vehicleThe line's pace: green, yellow or red (see below).
Label joined to the brackets by a lineThe line's name, always shown, and in small type its delay (+N') if it is late or else its speed. Labels take a free corner so they never cover one another.
Grey pauseStopped (under 2 mph).
Red tag with a speedometer and "72 mph"Speeding: 65 mph or more. It pulses slowly to catch the eye and says by how much.
FadedIts last position is more than 5 minutes old.

Hover a vehicle for a card with its line and pace, the driver, the vehicle, the group and the day's trips, the speed and the time of its last position.

Always moving#

The Driver app reports its position about every 30 seconds. Between one report and the next, a moving vehicle keeps going along the road of its leg, at the speed it reported, for at most 45 seconds. When the real position arrives, the icon is corrected smoothly, without jumps. A stopped vehicle, or one with no recent position, stays where it was reported: no movement is invented.

Following a vehicle#

Select a vehicle and press Follow in the selection bar: the map zooms in and the camera keeps the vehicle in the middle while it moves, at any zoom, street level included (zoom in with the mouse wheel). Dragging the map by hand only pauses it until you let go. Following stops when you press Follow again, press Esc or select another vehicle.

Pace: green, yellow, red#

The vehicle's brackets and the road it is about to drive (pickup to drop-off) take one of three colours. Nothing needs switching on: it always works.

ColourWhat it means
🟢 GreenAll in order: on time.
🟡 YellowSlightly late.
🔴 RedBadly late.

Where the colour comes from (default, free)#

Every minute, Raphael works out the estimated arrival of each line with a trip under way and compares it with the promised time. It does not ask Google, so it costs nothing.

The estimate comes from two sources, and the later one is used:

  1. The Driver's ETA. Each time the driver performs a stop, the app works out with traffic the ETA of the next two stops (and the pickup's on "Start trip"). Very accurate at that moment, but it does not change until the next perform.
  2. The live estimate. Now + miles to go ÷ real average speed over the last 10 minutes (distance driven between the GPS fixes ÷ time). Miles to go are measured along the route's stored road or, without one, as a straight line × 1.3. If the vehicle gets stuck between two performs, this estimate grows minute by minute even though the Driver's ETA still says "on time".

What it is compared with:

  • Heading to pick the patient up → the pickup time, and also the appointment time: what the pickup slips is added to the Driver's drop-off ETA. The worse of the two delays wins.
  • With the patient on board → the appointment time.

A vehicle at a standstill is counted at 5 mph, so the ETA is long rather than infinite; one waiting at the stop has almost no miles left, so it does not blow up.

ColourRule
🟢 Green5 minutes late or less (or early).
🟡 Yellow6 to 15 minutes late.
🔴 RedMore than 15 minutes late.

A line with no trip under way, or no promised time to compare with, shows green.

With the "Delays on each route" layer (Google, with a cost)#

Tick Delays on each route and the colour stops coming from punctuality and comes from Google's live traffic: how many minutes the traffic adds to the vehicle's road to its stop.

ColourRule
🟢 GreenTraffic adds under 3 minutes (or under 15 %).
🟡 YellowIt adds 3 minutes or more and at least 15 % more time.
🔴 RedIt adds 10 minutes or more, or 50 % more with at least 5 minutes (a jam, an accident).

Untick it and the colours go back to punctuality at once, with no cost. Under the legend in the left panel the map always says where the colour comes from right now.

The "Delays on each route" layer and its cost#

⚠️ This layer asks Google and costs money, billed to your own Google Maps key.

When it can be used. It is disabled (grey) until you save a valid Google Maps key in POWER UP (bottom right). On save, Raphael tests it with one request: if Google does not accept it, it is not saved and you are told why. The key needs the Routes API enabled in Google Cloud.

What is asked. Google Routes API (Compute Routes Pro, traffic aware): one request per line on route, from its vehicle to the stop it is heading for, every 10 minutes while the layer is ticked.

What it costs. The first 5,000 requests each month are free; then about US$10 per 1,000.

Lines on routeRequests an hourIn a 12-hour dayIn a month (every day)
742504~15,000 (~US$100)
15901,080~32,000 (~US$270)
301802,160~65,000 (~US$600)

Before it starts, Raphael shows this same calculation with your lines at that moment and asks whether to go on.

Delays TomTom (trial)#

The same as Delays on each route, with TomTom's live traffic instead of Google's and your TomTom key (saved in POWER UP and tested on save). It asks TomTom Routing API (Calculate Route with traffic): one request per line on route every 10 minutes. TomTom's pricing page lists 2,500 free requests a day, then about US$0.75 per 1,000; check your plan. Before it starts it shows the calculation with your lines. It never runs together with the Google layer: ticking one unticks the other.

The other two layers have no cost per line:

  • Routes in progress draws each line's road with the shapes Raphael already stored when the trips were routed. It does not ask Google.
  • Traffic (Google) paints the streets' traffic. It is part of the Google map itself and is not billed separately.

The Details card#

Details in the selection bar, or a double click on a vehicle, opens its card. At the top: the vehicle in Raphael livery, drawn by its kind (AMB minivan, WCH van with a ramp, STR van with a stretcher), the line, the vehicle, the driver's name, the group and the capacities. Below: the progress of the trip under way, its arrival time and the day's facts.

The panels#

  • Left: Filters (which groups show), On the map (how many lines in each group), Layers and Legend.
  • Right: Today (the day's figures) and Drivers.
  • Drivers: one row per driver who has reported today: name and line, hours on shift, trips done of the day's, and miles. The bar shows trips done while the day runs and, after the Pull-in, the shift split into with a patient, waiting and empty. Click a driver to select their line, as a click on their vehicle does: the row stays marked.

The TODAY card#

The ring is the whole day: each colour is a part and the number in the middle is how much of the day is done (canceled trips do not count towards it). Below, one row per part; they add up to the total and follow the same rule as Home. They are read every minute. Pressing a row:

RowWhat it opens
CompletedToday's Home, only the finished ones
In progressThe list of trips under way, here on the map
Not startedToday's Home, only those with a run that have not started
CancelledToday's Home, only the canceled ones
Without a runDispatch's open list for today: what is left to route

In Home, Clear all removes that filter. A row at 0 cannot be pressed.

The In progress list#

Each card says the patient, where the trip stands (on the way to the pickup, at the door or on board), the promised time, the line, the driver, the trip number and whether it is on time or late. Press one to select its vehicle and see its road. The X closes the list.

The next stop and the drive home#

Next stop, in Details, is the stop's name as Dispatch lists it, with the patient. Before leaving it says Pull-out, and with everything done, Pull-in: the map then draws the road to the garage. Stops of canceled trips do not count.

The top buttons#

Six round buttons in the centre. Hover one and it grows, with a card naming it and saying what it does.

ButtonWhat it does
Street ViewClick it, then click a street (streets with imagery show in blue). Inside Street View the panels step aside; leave with Exit Street View or Esc.
Map viewOpens a small pad to zoom and move the map step by step.
Full screenThe map in its own window over the whole screen, panels and dock included. Esc leaves.
Fit the vehiclesFrames every vehicle shown.
Hide panelsLeaves only the map, with no window on top. Bring them back with the same button or the H key.
Own windowMoves the map to a separate window (for a second screen) and back to the tab. The map reloads in a few seconds when it moves.

The dock#

  • Search: lines (by line, driver, plate or vehicle), trips (by Trip Id or patient) and places.
  • Microphone: voice control (needs the OpenAI key in POWER UP).
  • Visual presets: seven looks for the map (keys 1 to 7) and the map type, including photorealistic 3D, where the vehicles show with the same icon and keep moving, the selected

vehicle shows its trip and stops, and Fit and Follow work the same. The map opens without labels by default.

POWER UP#

Bottom right. Today OpenAI (the microphone), Google Maps (the Delays layer) and TomTom (Delays TomTom, on trial) work. Anthropic, Grok, OpenRouter, Mapbox, HERE and Amazon are listed at the end, disabled, until they are available. Keys are kept in your Windows user's appsettings.json on this computer and are never shown again.
