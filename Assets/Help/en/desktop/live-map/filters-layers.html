Filters, counts, layers and legend

The left panel: which lines show, how many there are, the four layers (and their cost) and the legend.

referenceNew in 1.23.0

The left panel decides what shows on the map. Each section folds with its – or + button.

FILTERS#

One box per group, with its color and how many lines it has. Ticked, its vehicles show. Only On route today is ticked when you open the map. It filters the DRIVERS list too.

GroupWhich lines
On route todayThey have trips today
Active, no tripsIn force, no trips today
Suspended todayWith a suspension that includes today
Out of serviceTheir vehicle or driver is out of service
ExpiredTheir to date has passed, or they have not started yet

If a line meets several, out of service rules first, then suspended, then expired.

ON THE MAP#

Counts what shows: Moving, Stopped, Speeding, Old position (over 5 minutes), No recent position (lines of shown groups that are missing) and Trips today. Information only, not pressed. It starts folded.

LAYERS#

They can be combined, except the two delays ones, which exclude each other.

LayerWhat it drawsCost
Routes in progressEach line's road to its next stop, with a chequered flag where it goes, or the garage if it is going back. No road if the driver has not started the next trip. Below, how many lines are driving and how many waitNone
Traffic (Google)Street traffic: green flowing, orange slow, red congested, dark red stoppedNone of its own
Delays on each routeEach vehicle's road with Google's live traffic and +N' with the minutes it adds. The pace then comes from trafficYes, on your Google key
Delays TomTom (trial)The same with TomTomYes, on your TomTom key

The two delays layers are grey until their key is saved in POWER UP. Ticking one shows the cost with your lines right now and asks; No leaves it off. They ask once per line on route every 10 minutes; not for a line going back to the garage. Unticked, the pace is punctuality again at once, at no cost.

Their state shows below: Asking…, No vehicle is on its way to a stop, No congestion or N of M routes delayed.

LEGEND#

It recalls the vehicle marks and the three pace colors. The last line, in italics, says where the color comes from now. The detail is in The vehicles.

What can go wrong#

What you seeWhat to do
An empty mapTick On route today. At 0, no trip has a run today
A delays layer in greySave its key in POWER UP
Routes in progress does not draw a lineIts driver has not started the next trip
