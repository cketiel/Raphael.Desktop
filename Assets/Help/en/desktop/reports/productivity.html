Fleet productivity — hours and miles per driver

How to see each driver's paid hours and miles per day over a date range, open a day's breakdown and save it as a PDF or to Excel.

how-toNew in 1.21.2

The Fleet productivity tab tells you how many hours each driver worked and how many miles they drove, day by day, over the date range you choose.

Getting the report#

  1. Open Reports and the Fleet productivity tab.
  2. Pick the start and end dates.
  3. Press Generate.
  4. If you like, tick one or more drivers or vehicles: the list changes at once, with no need to generate again. With none ticked, everyone comes out. Whoever has data in those dates goes first; the rest is dimmed. Under each filter you see what is ticked, with an X to clear it.

At the top are the period's totals: driver-days, paid hours, odometer miles, the share of miles with a patient, and trips completed. Below, one line per driver and day.

What each column means#

  • Paid shift: from the allowed start to the allowed end, in your clock. If the driver started earlier than their first trip justified, or finished later than the drive back to the garage justified, that part is not paid.
  • Paid: that shift's hours, as h:mm.
  • Odometer: the miles between the Pull-out and the Pull-in readings. This is the figure that counts.
  • Loaded: the share of those miles driven with at least one patient on board.
  • Reliability: OK when nothing stands out; ! or !! when something deserves a look. Hover it to see what: marks pressed far from the stop, hours outside the allowed window, miles off any route, or an odometer that does not match the GPS. It never changes the figures, it only warns.

What is paid#

If you are an administrator, the report also says how much is paid:

  • Rate: the one the driver had that day, hourly ($15.00/h) or per mile ($1.00/mi).
  • Amount: the rate times the paid hours, or times the miles with a patient on board (two patients at once count two miles). Hover over it to see the sum.
  • Rows are grouped by driver, with the subtotal in each group's header.
  • At the top, the Total pay tile, and under the list, the grand total, split into what is paid hourly and what is paid per mile.

Each driver's rate is changed in A driver's pay rate.

Opening a day#

Click a line. A card opens underneath with three parts, each with its coloured bar and legend:

  • Hours, minute by minute: with a patient, inspection, waiting for the hour, waiting for the patient, driving empty and other. These six add up to the paid shift. After them, apart: the real shift, what was not paid, and patient-hours (two patients for an hour count two).
  • Miles: odometer, loaded, empty, GPS, planned, passenger-miles and off-route.
  • Reliability: a green note when all is well, or one card for each thing worth a look.

Saving it#

  • Save as PDF: a landscape page with one line per day and the totals, to print or send.
  • Save as Excel: every figure in its own column, hours as durations that add up, and a totals row made of formulas. This is the one for payroll.
  • With amounts, both also carry each day's rate and amount, a subtotal per driver and the grand total. In Excel the amount is a formula (rate × units).

Why it matters

These hours are what drivers get paid. The paid shift does not reward starting early or stretching the drive back, and the reliability column points at the days worth a look before they are paid, instead of the mistake surfacing in a complaint weeks later.

A day appears when its driver closes the shift

A day's figures are worked out when the driver does the Pull-in, or in the nightly pass at 3:00. Today does not appear yet. Days from before the shift metrics existed have no data: the report says so instead of showing zeros.

Office staff only

A user with the driver role does not see this tab. Each Provider sees only its own drivers.
