Fleet productivity — the screen

What the hours and miles report is, every zone of the screen, every column, every button and what to do in each situation.

referenceNew in 1.21.2

Fleet productivity tells, per driver and day, how many hours are paid, how many miles they drove and how much they are paid. It is the basis of the drivers' payroll. The office uses it: dispatchers and administrators. Only an administrator sees the amounts, and each Provider sees only its own drivers.

Open Reports · What do the warnings mean?

The words you need#

WordWhat it is
ShiftOne driver, one line and one day. It exists once the driver does the Pull-in
Pull-out / Pull-inLeaving the garage and coming back
Arrive / PerformReaching a stop / doing the pickup or the dropoff
Real shiftFrom when the driver started to the Pull-in
Paid shiftThe part of the real shift that is paid
Miles with a patientEach mile times the patients on board: a mile with 2 patients counts 2. They are the ones paid per mile
Occupied milesThe distance the vehicle drove with anyone on board, counted once. Not paid
Empty milesOdometer − occupied miles
WarningSomething worth a look. It changes no figure

Getting the report#

  1. Open Reports and the Fleet productivity tab.
  2. Pick the start and end dates. At most 400 days.
  3. Press GENERATE.
  4. If you like, tick Drivers or Vehicles. The list changes at once, with no need to generate again. With none ticked, everyone comes out. Whoever has data goes first; the rest is dimmed. The X below clears it.

A day appears once its driver does the Pull-in. A pass at 3:00 picks up what arrived late from yesterday.

ZoneWhat it showsTopic

Click a row to open its detail, and click it again to hide it. F1 opens the topic of the zone under the mouse.

The three helps of this screen#

HelpWhat forHow it opens
? icons beside each figureThat figure: what it is, its formula, whether it is paid and the example with that shift's numbersOne click
What do the warnings mean?Each warning: drawing, sum, when it goes off, whether it affects pay and what to do. The values that set them off are edited thereLeft panel, detail or any ? icon
F1The whole report, told in partsThe F1 key

The three link to each other: a ? icon leads to the help and to its warnings, and the warnings window leads to the help.

The cards#

CardWhat it says
Driver-daysHow many closed shifts there are, and of how many drivers
Paid hoursTheir sum, and the average per shift. Paid to drivers paid by the hour
Odometer milesTheir sum, and the average per shift. They are not paid
Miles with a patientMiles × patients on board. Paid to drivers paid per mile. Below, the occupancy: occupied miles ÷ odometer
Trips completedPickup and dropoff performed
Total payWhat those shifts cost: what goes by the hour and what goes per mile

The columns#

ColumnWhat it is
Date, Driver, Vehicle · lineWhose shift it is and when
Paid shiftFrom when to when it is paid, in your clock
PaidThose hours, as h:mm
OdometerPull-in − Pull-out. The mile figure that rules
OccupancyOccupied miles ÷ odometer: the share of the road with anyone on board
TripsThose completed
Rate, AmountWhat they earn and what that day adds up to. Administrators only. Hover the amount to see the sum
ReliabilityOK, or how many warnings. Red, almost certainly an error; amber, worth a look; blue, information

Saving it#

  • SAVE AS PDF: a landscape page, one line per shift, subtotals and total.
  • SAVE AS EXCEL: every figure in its column, hours that add up and totals made of formulas. It is the one for payroll. The amount is a formula: rate × units.

Both save what is in the list, with the filters on. While it is saved the screen waits and you can Cancel; when it ends, the app says so and asks whether you want to open the file.

What to do in each situation#

I want to…Do this
Pay a fortnightPick the dates → GENERATE → check the warnings → SAVE AS EXCEL
See one driver onlyTick them in Drivers
Know why they earn those hoursClick their row → Calculation, in the hours
Know why they have those milesClick their row → Calculation, in the miles
Know what a figure isPress the ? beside it
Correct an odometerClick the row → warning → Correct reading (administrator)
Change when a warning goes offWhat do the warnings mean? → pick the warning → change its limit → Save limits (platform administrator)
Know who did not closeOpen the red strip below
Change a driver's ratePay rate

The values that set the warnings off can be edited

The platform administrator changes them in What do the warnings mean?: each warning has its limit there and can be turned off. How to do it: Changing the limits.

What can go wrong#

What you seeWhat happens and what to do
A shift is missingThe driver did not do the Pull-in. Look at the red strip and ask for it
"Nothing in these dates for what is ticked"A filter hides it. Clear it with its X
The total miles are hugeA mistyped reading. Look for the red warning
"The file could not be saved"It is open in another program or the folder cannot be written to. Close it or change the name
You do not see the amountsOnly an administrator does
"The warnings could not be loaded"The report is valid. Generate it again

Why it matters

Drivers are paid with this screen. One minute or one digit too many is money, and it shows here before it is paid.
