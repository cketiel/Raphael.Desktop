Impossible and loose trips report

The trips that came impossible or loose, what was adjusted and the proof to claim.

how-toNew in 1.23.15

Gathers the trips of some dates whose hours came impossible or loose: the broker's original hour, the adjusted one, who adjusted it and when, and what happened. A trip that came impossible is always here, even if it was adjusted.

The steps#

  1. In Reports, open Impossible and loose trips, choose the dates (93 days at most) and press Generate.
  2. Filter by funding source and by proof. Choosing a trip shows below the sum, what was received and what happened.
  3. Claim (PDF): choose a funding source first. It is in English, with its own trips only. Administrators generate it.
  4. Excel: every figure of what you have on screen.

What proof each one has#

ProofWhat it meansIn the claim?
By factsThe driver was at the pickup on time and the appointment was still missedYes
By calculationThe hours contradict each other, or more than 5 minutes are missing with a road priced by GoogleYes
Arguable5 minutes short or less, or the road is estimatedOnly if you choose it
Do not claimThe pickup was late tooOnly if you choose it

The patient's name is left out of the files unless you tick the box.

For the broker: late is arriving more than 15 minutes after the appointment; 1 to 15 minutes shows as "admitted". Loose trips are not claimed: they explain why the pickup was moved.

Origin: where the trip came from. Funding source file and integration come from the broker and can be claimed. Typed in the office was written in the office, even with the broker's funding source: it is not claimed and never goes in the PDF. Unknown origin: trips created before 09/05/2026, when the import left no mark yet. The From the broker filter keeps only those claimable by origin.

Claim what can be proven

A claim with doubtful cases casts doubt on the good ones. That is why they stay out unless you ask for them.

What can go wrong#

It says some appointments are not checked. They are from before this existed. An administrator presses Check the pending ones.

"Original hours not confirmed". The trip already existed when Raphael began keeping the hours received.
