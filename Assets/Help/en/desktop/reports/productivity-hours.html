The hours — how the paid shift is worked out

The exact rule of the hours that are paid: allowed start and end, the six groups of minutes, which figure is paid and why, with a full example.

referenceNew in 1.23.0

A driver paid by the hour is paid the paid shift: the part of their shift the route justifies. It is worked out with an exact rule, with no margin, the same for everyone.

The formula#

Allowed start = first stop's promised hour − drive from the garage to it − preparation − inspection

Allowed end = last stop's Perform + drive back to the garage + inspection

Paid from / until
Paid startThe later of the real start and the allowed start
Paid endThe earlier of the real Pull-in and the allowed end

Paid shift = paid end − paid start

Where each number comes from#

NumberWhat it is
Promised hourThe pickup hour of the first real stop. Without one, its appointment
Drive outGarage → first stop, at the hour they had to leave. The server works it out
Preparation20 minutes, fixed
InspectionThe minutes paid for each inspection: 10 from the factory, and only if the Provider has the inspection on. Without it, 0
Last stop's PerformThe real hour the last trip ended
Drive backLast stop → garage. The server works it out and keeps it when that Perform is done; nobody can stretch it from the phone
Real startThe beginning of the Pull-out inspection. Without an inspection, the Pull-out
Real endThe Pull-in

A cancelled trip nobody drove to is not a stop. If the driver reached the door, it is.

A full example#

The first pickup is at 08:00, with a 34-minute drive and the inspection on. The driver started at 06:08 and their last Perform was at 16:41, with a 31-minute drive back. They did the Pull-in at 18:10.

StepSumResult
Allowed start08:00 − 34 − 20 − 10 min06:56
Paid startthe later of 06:08 and 06:5606:56
Not paid at the start06:56 − 06:0848 min
Allowed end16:41 + 31 + 10 min17:22
Paid endthe earlier of 18:10 and 17:2217:22
Not paid at the end18:10 − 17:2248 min
Paid shift17:22 − 06:5610:26
Real shift18:10 − 06:0812:02

10:26 paid + 1:36 not paid = 12:02 real. At $15.00 an hour, $156.50 are paid.

Had they started late, at 07:10, they would be paid from 07:10: it is paid from when they arrive.

This sum, with each day's numbers, is in Calculation: three bars —real shift, not paid and paid shift— each with its total, and the sums of the start and the end below.

The six groups: where the shift went#

Each minute of the paid shift falls in one group only. If it fits two, it stays in the first of the table. The six add up to the paid shift exactly. They are only its breakdown: they are not paid separately. What is paid is the whole paid shift, wherever it went.

#GroupWhich minutes
1With patientFrom the pickup's Perform to the dropoff's. Two trips at once count once
2InspectionWhat each inspection lasted, up to its maximum
3Wait for the hourArrived before the window opened: 15 min before the hour on the way out, 5 on a return
4Wait for the patientWith the window open, until Perform. At the dropoff, from arriving to Perform
5Driving emptyDriving to a stop with nobody on board, from leaving the previous one or pressing Start
6OtherThe rest: free time between trips, lunch, the long part of an inspection

So free time between trips takes nothing off: it is inside the paid shift.

What comes apart#

FigureWhat it isPaid?
Real shiftFrom the real start to the Pull-inNo
Not paidWhat is outside the allowed start and endNo
Patient-hoursOne hour with two patients is two. Outside the sumNo
TripsThose completedNo

The "Shift outside the allowed hours" warning#

Unpaid minutes are left out all of them, one by one. The warning deducts nothing: it only calls your attention when they exceed its limit, 15 minutes from the factory. With 10 unpaid minutes there is no warning, and they are not paid either.

What can be adjusted#

The platform administrator changes the minutes paid for each inspection in What do the warnings mean? → Shift outside the allowed hours. It applies to the shifts worked out from then on, today's included; those already worked out do not change, not even with the 3:00 pass: each keeps the minutes it used. The 20-minute preparation does not change.

The values that set the warnings off can be edited

The platform administrator changes them in What do the warnings mean?: each warning has its limit there and can be turned off. How to do it: Changing the limits.

What can go wrong#

What you seeWhat happens
"Shift end without a limit"A stop was left without a Perform and there is no drive back: it is paid until the Pull-in
"The allowed start could not be worked out"The first stop has no hour: it is paid from the real start
They earn less than the driver saysOpen the calculation: nearly always they started before the allowed hour
The shift ended past midnightIt is measured until the hour it ended, on the next day
