Production Report — a period's production

How to get production by dates, Funding Source, route, driver and vehicle, preview it as a PDF and save it to Excel.

how-to

The Production Report tells you which trips were done in a period, for whom, and with which route, driver and vehicle. You preview it as a PDF and save it to Excel.

When you need it#

  • When you close a week or a month, to bill each Funding Source.
  • To review the work of one driver or one vehicle.
  • If you sign in with the driver role, to see your own production.

The steps#

  1. Open Reports and the "Production Report" tab.
  2. Under "Date Range", pick the start date and the end date.
  3. Pick the filters you want. All four work the same way:
    • "Funding Sources"
    • "Vehicle Routes"
    • "Drivers"
    • "Vehicles"

Open the list and tick one or several. Each filter's "[ SELECT ALL … ]" box ticks or unticks all of them at once.

  1. Press "GENERATE PREVIEW" to see the report as a PDF on screen.
  2. Press "SAVE AS EXCEL" to save the same report to an Excel file.

How the filters combine:

  • A filter with nothing ticked does not narrow anything: everything comes in.
  • If you tick in several filters, a trip has to match all of them at once. For example, driver A and route R5 give only R5's trips while A is its driver.

The lists include everything: active, inactive and suspended routes, drivers and vehicles. A period's production is always shown, even if that route no longer works today.

If you sign in with the driver role: you see only your route, your name and your route's vehicle, already ticked. You pick the dates and generate the report.

Why it matters

This report is what gets billed and what gets paid. A badly combined filter gives no error: it gives a report with fewer trips, and the gap is found later by somebody who cannot reconcile an invoice.

What can go wrong#

  • "No data found for the selected filters." No trip matches all the filters at once. Check that you did not combine a driver with a route that is not theirs, and widen the dates.
  • An old trip shows a different driver or vehicle. The report's driver and vehicle are the ones the route has assigned today, not the ones that did the trip. If the route changed driver or vehicle, earlier trips show the new one, and the driver or vehicle filter counts them that way too. To review a period in which assignments changed, filter by route.
  • A route, driver or vehicle is missing from the list. The lists load when the tab opens. If somebody has just added it, open Reports again.
