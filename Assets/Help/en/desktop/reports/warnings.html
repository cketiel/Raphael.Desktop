The report's warnings — all thirteen

How Fleet productivity's warnings and their cases are seen, what each one means, when it goes off, whether it affects pay, what to do and how their limits are set.

referenceNew in 1.23.15

A warning points at something worth a look. It never changes hours, miles or pay. The explanation with drawings is in What do the warnings mean?.

Seeing them#

  1. The Reliability column says OK or how many warnings the shift has. The colour is the gravest one's.
  2. Click the row. On the right there is a card per warning, with its figure: 9 cases, 0:34 not paid, 160 mi… Above, how many warnings and cases there are in all.
  3. Press See detail: why it warns, when it goes off, each case numbered, the total and what to do.
  4. On a mark far from the stop, rest the mouse on the line: the stop and where the GPS had the vehicle are drawn. The cases' hour is the route's.

Shifts with no Pull-in have no row: they are in the red strip, under the list.

ColourMeans
RedAlmost certainly an error
AmberWorth a look
BlueInformation only

Almost certainly an error#

WarningWhen it goes offWhat to do
Impossible Pull-in readingMore than 2 times the GPS miles (or the planned ones), and 25 mi apartCorrect the Pull-in
Impossible Pull-out readingMore than 500 mi per day since the last sensible reading, up to 7 daysCorrect the Pull-out, or the route's vehicle
The odometer goes backwardsBelow the previous reading, or Pull-in below Pull-outCorrect the one that does not fit
Shift not closed24 hours with no Pull-in. It is not in the payAsk the driver for the Pull-in. It is accepted the next day

Worth a look#

WarningWhen it goes offWhat to do
Marked far from the stopArrive or Perform more than 0.2 mi away. Each press is a caseSee the drawings; if it is always the same address, fix its position
Shift outside the allowed hoursMore than 15 unpaid minutes. How it is worked outSee the calculation and talk to the driver if it repeats
Off-route milesMore than 100 mi since the last readingFind out who moved it, or correct the Pull-out
Odometer and GPS do not matchMore than 20% apartSee whether the reading is rounded
Zero miles with trips donePull-in = Pull-out, with a tripAsk for the real reading and correct it
Possibly the wrong vehicleTwo routes, one vehicle and incompatible readingsCorrect the route's vehicle, not the readings
Shift too longMore than 14 real hoursSee whether they forgot the Pull-in

Information only#

WarningWhen it goes offDoes it affect pay?
Incomplete GPSA leg without GPS, measured by its planned distanceYes, for a driver paid per mile: it is estimated
Shift end without a limitThere is no drive back: usually a Perform is missingYes: it is paid until the Pull-in

The numbers are the factory ones, and can be edited in What do the warnings mean?, where those in force are read. See Changing the limits, below. A reading is compared with the last one that made sense, never with a mistake: after an extra digit, the right reading is right.

Correcting from a warning#

An administrator sees Correct reading on the odometer and mile warnings. Odometer history opens all the vehicle's readings. See A vehicle's odometer.

Changing the limits#

The platform administrator only. They apply to every Provider.

  1. Open What do the warnings mean? and pick the warning.
  2. Change the limit, or untick Warning on.
  3. Read how many shifts of the last 30 days would be marked.
  4. Press Save limits. Factory settings puts them back; save afterwards.

Who changed it and when are kept. A new limit also changes what is marked on old days.

What can go wrong#

What you seeWhat happens
"The warnings could not be loaded"The report is valid. Generate it again
There is no "Correct reading"Only an administrator sees it
A mark with no GPS positionThe phone gave no position within the two minutes around it

Why it matters

One extra digit in an odometer distorts the whole fleet's miles, and a shift with no Pull-in never reaches payroll. The warning shows it before anyone pays or decides with that number.
