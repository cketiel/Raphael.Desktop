Notes for the driver

How to keep the notes about a patient's home, the facilities and the patient once, so they reach every trip by themselves.

how-toNew in 1.14.0

The pickup and drop-off comments are the driver's guide. You no longer have to write them every day: notes are kept on the place (the patient's home or the facility) and on the patient, and the system puts them on every new or imported trip by itself.

When you need it#

  • When you know something the driver must always know: dogs at the house, a gate with a code, the emergency door, a patient who only speaks English.
  • When a trip shows the notes icon in orange: the file brought new notes nobody has reviewed yet.

Where to open them#

  • On the trip form, Pickup and Dropoff tabs: the ⤢ button in the corner of the comment.
  • In the trips grid: the notes icon on each row. Orange when there are notes to review, grey otherwise.
  • On the Dispatch tab, when editing a trip: the same ⤢ button by each comment.

The trip must be saved: notes are kept on the places of a trip that already exists. When you book a new trip, the comments fill themselves with the notes that already exist as soon as you pick the patient and the addresses, so you only have to review them.

The three kinds of note#

NoteReachesExample
Of the place (or of one entrance)every trip to that place, any patient"Park at level 2", "Emergency: door 3"
Of the patientall their trips, wherever they go"Speaks only English", "Needs help to board"
This trip onlythis trip and no other"Today, third floor"

Patient notes are text: they do not change how trips are routed.

Why it matters

This guide is all the driver has in front of them. If the gate has a code or the right door is the emergency one and the note does not reach them, they drive around, arrive late, and the patient misses their dialysis slot. Keeping the note once on the place means nobody has to type it again every day.

What the driver reads#

The green box at the top, "What the driver reads here", is exactly what they will see at that stop: this trip's note first, then the entrance's, the place's, the patient's, and whatever the file sent that no note covers yet. It is the same text the comment field shows.

Steps#

  1. Open the trip's notes.
  2. Pick the Pickup, Drop-off or Patient tab.
  3. Type in New note for this place… (or for this entrance, or about the patient) and press Add or Enter.
  4. For something that holds for one day, type it in This trip only and press Save. If it turns out to hold for good, press Keep it for the place or Keep it for the patient.

Every change is saved at once and reaches the trips from today on that have not finished. Past trips keep what they carried.

Notes to review#

When an import file, a clinic or an integrator brings a new sentence, it is kept as a place note to review (amber background). The driver already sees it. You decide:

  • Confirm (✓): it is right and stays.
  • Edit: correct it; saving it confirms it.
  • Remove: it held for one trip only. It does not come back even if tomorrow's file repeats it.

Whatever the file repeats and is already kept is not duplicated.

The place#

Each stop is matched to a place by itself: the patient's home if it is the address on their record, or a facility shared by every patient that goes there. A large hospital is one place with several entrances.

  • Rename the place (pencil): for example, "Jackson Memorial Hospital".
  • Name this entrance (door): for example, "Emergency".
  • Use another place (arrows): if the address was matched to the wrong facility, search for the right one and pick it.

If something goes wrong#

  • "Save the trip first": it is a new trip. Save it and open the notes again.
  • I cannot type in "This trip only": the trip is past or closed. The place's and the patient's notes can still change.
  • Another trip at the same place does not show the new note in the grid: the grid updates when the day is loaded again. The driver already has it.

What the driver sees and can do#

In the Driver app, each stop's detail shows the same text as the comment field, under NOTES. Tapping it opens that stop's notes page:

  • They see what they read at that stop, this trip's note, the entrance's, the place's and the patient's notes, and what the clinic or the file sent.
  • They can add, edit, confirm and remove any note, as the office can. What they write shows up here with the source Driver, and reaches every trip still to come.
  • With no signal nothing is saved: the app tells them, and they try again when they have coverage.

Look over the notes from the Driver now and then: they are what is learnt on the road.

What others see#

The clinic, the patient's app and integrators see only what they or the file sent, never the office's notes: they describe the patient's home and health.
