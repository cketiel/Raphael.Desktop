Compliance — driver, vehicle and dispatcher documents

Review, approve or reject documents, see what expires, upload one for someone else and notify drivers.

how-to

Compliance keeps the documents that must be in order: each driver's, each vehicle's and each dispatcher's. A document only counts once an administrator approves it.

When you need it#

  • A driver uploaded a document from the app and is waiting for approval.
  • You want to know what expires in the next 30, 60 or 90 days before it does.
  • You have the paper in your hand and upload it yourself for the driver or the vehicle.
  • You want to tell one or several drivers to renew something.

The tabs#

  • "To review": what is waiting, with "View" to open it. Only an administrator approves or rejects; a dispatcher sees what is waiting but does not decide.
  • "Expiring": approved documents expiring "Within" 30, 60 or 90 days. Expired ones are listed too, first.
  • "Staff": drivers and dispatchers, with their counts and next expiry. Select one and their documents show below.
  • "Vehicles": the same, per vehicle. The five photos go in one document.
  • "My documents": your own.

The steps#

Approve or reject (administrator):

  1. In "To review", press "View" and check the document and its expiration date.
  2. Press "Approve", or "Reject" and write the reason. Whoever uploaded it reads that reason.

Upload or renew a document:

  1. Select the person or vehicle in "Staff" or "Vehicles".
  2. On its document, press "Upload" or "Renew".
  3. Choose a file for every part (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 10 MB each) and the expiration date as printed on the document.
  4. Press "Send for approval". If you are an administrator and have checked it, tick "Approve it now (you have checked it)".

While it is in review you can fix it with "Correct (still in review)": the date, or only the files you want to replace. "History" shows every version, who uploaded it and who reviewed it.

Download a document:

  1. In any list ("To review", "Expiring", or the documents of "Staff", "Vehicles" and "My documents"), press "Download", the arrow icon next to "View".
  2. If the document has one file, choose where to save it. If it has several (a vehicle's photos), choose a folder and they are all saved there.

The file is named after the document and its owner. What you save stays on your computer: treat it as a driver's personal document.

The screen updates itself. When a driver uploads a document from the app, or another user uploads, corrects, approves or rejects one, you see it straight away, without pressing anything. What you had ticked and selected stays as it was.

From a notification: opening a document notice (double-click in the notification center's Compliance tab) shows the document as it is now: status, expiry, who uploaded it and who reviewed it. "View document" opens it, and "Open the document in Compliance" brings you here with its owner selected and the document marked with a yellow bell. If a newer version was uploaded since the notice, the card says so.

Notify drivers:

  1. In "Expiring" or "Staff", tick the drivers or their documents.
  2. Press "Notify selected". The text comes ready, with the document and its date when it is just one; you can change it.
  3. Press "Send". It arrives by push, in Alerts, and stays on their Dashboard until read.

From Users and Vehicles, the "Documents" column sums up each row, and its icon opens Compliance right on that person or vehicle.

Why it matters

A driver with an expired license or insurance should not carry patients. Compliance warns before it expires, but the warning is useless if what gets uploaded is never reviewed.

What can go wrong#

  • "Only an administrator can approve or reject." You signed in as a dispatcher. You see what is waiting, but an administrator has to decide.
  • "Tick at least one driver…" You ticked only vehicles or dispatchers, and they have no driver app to receive the notice.
  • "The file is larger than 10 MB." Shrink the photo or PDF and choose it again.
  • "The expiration date has already passed." An expired document is not uploaded as valid: the renewed one is needed.
  • A PDF does not open inside Raphael. It opens in the computer's viewer; if there is none, its folder opens.
