Admin — Providers

The providers in the operation and the timezone their trips run in.

reference

This page is not complete yet

What is here is checked against the product. The rest of the form —name, contact, logo— will be documented later.

The providers in the operation and the timezone their trips run in. To edit one, open it from the Providers tab in Admin.

Work mode#

Only Raphael's administrator sees it, and only on a Provider that already exists. There are two modes:

  • Works in Raphael: its users enter the Desktop and run their trips there. It can be a client company of Raphael.
  • External (Booking portal only): it runs its trips in another system. Its users only enter the Booking portal, to see and export the trips the clinics give it.

A Provider that a clinic contracts from Booking is born External and without users. To have it work inside Raphael, switch it to Works in Raphael and give it Desktop users.

Switching one to External asks you to confirm: from their next sign-in its users stop entering the Desktop, and if it was a client company it stops being one.

Why it matters

When a Provider switches to Works in Raphael, its users stay active with the password they have, and an External Provider's users may have been created by a clinic. If a clinic knows that password, it could enter the Desktop and see trips of other clinics' patients. Before switching the mode, check its users in Admin → Users and deactivate, or change the password of, those who should not get in.

Client company of Raphael (tenant)#

The Client company of Raphael (tenant) box turns the Provider into a company that bought Raphael. Its users only see and change their own company's fleet, routes, payers and people, plus the common catalog. Give it users with that Provider.

Only Raphael's administrator sees it, and only while the Provider works in Raphael. On an External one it does not appear: it has no fleet here to isolate. If you need it to be one, switch its work mode first; you can do both in the same save.

A Provider with no users cannot receive trips#

To give a trip to a Provider, it must have at least one active user. Until it has one, it does not appear in Home's Provider selector and the server refuses to give it trips.

Why it matters

With no users there is nobody to see the trip, so nobody does it. The patient would be left without a ride with nobody noticing.

A Provider a clinic contracts from Booking is born with no users. To be able to give it trips, create one in Admin → Users with that Provider. More in Home's frequently asked questions.

Clinics that contracted it#

On an existing Provider, the form lists the clinics that contracted it. They are not changed here: they are corrected from each clinic's file, in Integrators.
