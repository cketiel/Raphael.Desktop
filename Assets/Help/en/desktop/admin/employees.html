Admin — Employees

The company's users, their roles and their permissions, and their AI access (MCP).

reference

Not written yet. This page exists so F1 has somewhere to land from the Employees tab in Admin, and so the help index already shows the full structure. The text will be written when that tab is documented. The Users and AI access (MCP) sections below are complete.

The company's users, their roles and their permissions.

Users#

The list of everybody who signs in to Raphael: office staff, drivers and other companies' users.

Find someone#

  • Tabs: All, Drivers, Office and External (integrator users, other companies' accounts). Each tab's number says how many there are with the search and filters you have set, so you know where to look before switching.
  • Search: type part of the name, username, email, phone or role. The list filters as you type.
  • Active only: on when the screen opens, so users who have left stay out of the way. Turn it off to see them; an inactive user carries the grey Inactive label.
  • Provider: Raphael staff only, to see one Provider's users.
  • The funnel button, inside the search box, clears the search and the filters at once.
  • Top right shows how many are shown out of how many, and the button to reload the list.

Each row's actions#

So the list reads well, each row has just two buttons:

  • The pencil edits the user. A double click on the row does the same.
  • The three dots ⋮ open a menu with everything else:
ActionWhat it doesFor whom
Pay rateOpens the driver's pay rate (how).Drivers, administrators only
DocumentsOpens Compliance on that person's documents.Everyone
Out of serviceTakes the driver out of service, with a reason.Active drivers in service
Back in servicePuts them back in service.Drivers out of service
Change passwordSets a new password for that user.Everyone
Delete userDeletes them, after you confirm.Everyone

The menu only shows the actions that make sense for that user: an office user gets no pay rate or service actions, and a driver in service gets no "Back in service".

The columns#

  • Name, with the initials in a circle: purple for a driver, grey otherwise.
  • Username, Role, Phone, Email and Provider (the last one for Raphael staff only).
  • Documents: where their documents stand; a click opens Compliance.
  • Active: green Active or grey Inactive.

On the Drivers tab the columns change: the Role goes (they are all drivers) and the Pay rate paid today appears (administrators only; a click opens it), with Service: In service or Out of service, with the reason when you hover.

AI access (MCP)#

When editing a user, Raphael's administrator sees the AI access (MCP) field. It decides whether that user can query their company's data from their AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or another), read-only, and at which level:

LevelWhat the assistant sees
NoneNothing (the default).
Summary (no patient data)Their company's operation, without the patients' name, address, phone, date of birth or e-mail.
Detail (with patient data)The same, with those details. Asks for BAA on record: the BAA with that AI vendor.
Full (set by Raphael only)Not chosen here.
  • The field does not appear when creating a user: create it first, then edit it.
  • Only Raphael's administrator sees it. Clinic users cannot have access.
  • Changing the level makes the user enrol their authenticator app again, with 7 days to do it.

All the detail (what the user needs, which assistants Raphael recognises and how a new one is approved) is in AI access (MCP). What the user does to connect their assistant is in Connect your AI assistant.
