Connect your AI assistant

Which AI assistant to use with Raphael, how to connect it from the web or from your computer, what you can ask it and what its limits are.

how-toNew in 1.18.1

You connect your AI assistant to Raphael once and, from then on, ask it for your company's reports in your own words: "how many hours did each driver work last week?".

When you need it#

  • Raphael's administrator has just given you AI access and you are using your assistant for the first time.
  • Your assistant asks you to sign in to Raphael again.
  • You change assistant or computer.

Before you start#

  1. AI access switched on for your Raphael user. Raphael's administrator grants it (see AI access (MCP)). You have 7 days from then to make the first connection.
  2. Your Raphael username and password.
  3. An authenticator app on your phone: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or another.
  4. The address of Raphael's MCP server, which your administrator gives you. It ends in /mcp. Do not open it in the browser: it is not a web page and you will see an error. It goes into your assistant.
  5. A subscription to an AI assistant that supports custom MCP connectors. Free accounts usually do not; check your plan.

Which assistant to use#

In this order of recommendation:

AssistantHow it connectsNotes
Claude (Anthropic)In the Claude app or at claude.ai, as a custom connectorTested with Raphael. Works on the web and in the desktop app.
Claude Code (Anthropic)From the computer's terminalFor technical users. Steps below.
ChatGPT (OpenAI)At chatgpt.com or in its app, as a custom connectorSome plans need developer mode switched on.
Grok (xAI)At grok.com, as a custom connector—
Gemini (Google)Only with Gemini CLI, from the computer's terminalGemini on the web cannot connect to Raphael yet.

Other assistants that work from the computer (for example, the terminal tools of Kimi or Qwen) also work: Raphael accepts any assistant that connects from your own machine.

Web or desktop: what changes#

From your computer (Claude Code, Gemini CLI and other terminal tools): at sign-in, Raphael hands the connection back to your own machine. That is why it accepts any tool, without a list.

From the web (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, grok.com): Raphael only accepts these four domains, verified against each vendor:

  • chatgpt.com
  • claude.ai
  • claude.com
  • grok.com

The Claude and ChatGPT desktop apps connect through those same websites, so they work too.

If you use a web assistant from another domain, the connection does not finish: your assistant tells you the domain "is not known" to Raphael yet. Ask Raphael's administrator to approve it, with the assistant's name. The technical team checks the domain belongs to the vendor and approves it; then you connect again and get in.

The steps#

With Claude (app or claude.ai)#

  1. Open Settings and the Connectors section.
  2. Press Add custom connector.
  3. Give it a name, for example Raphael, and paste the address of Raphael's MCP server. Leave the OAuth fields (Client ID and secret) empty: Claude registers itself.
  4. Press Connect. Raphael's sign-in page opens: continue with Raphael's sign-in, below.
  5. In a new chat, switch on the Raphael connector in the tools menu next to the message box.

With ChatGPT#

  1. In Settings, go to the connectors or apps section. If your plan asks for it, switch on developer mode first.
  2. Create a new connector with a name (Raphael) and the address of Raphael's MCP server. For authentication, choose OAuth.
  3. Save and connect it. Raphael's page opens: continue with Raphael's sign-in, below.
  4. In a new chat, switch on the Raphael connector.

With Grok (grok.com)#

  1. Open the Connectors section and choose New Connector, Custom.
  2. Give it a name and paste the address of Raphael's MCP server.
  3. Connect it and continue with Raphael's sign-in, below.

With Claude Code (terminal)#

  1. In a terminal, register the server: claude mcp add --transport http raphael <address of Raphael's MCP server>
  2. Open Claude Code (claude), type /mcp, choose raphael and press Authenticate.
  3. The browser opens Raphael's page: continue with Raphael's sign-in, below.

The Code tab of the Claude desktop app cannot sign in to a custom MCP server yet. Use the app's connector (above) or Claude Code in the terminal.

With Gemini CLI (terminal)#

  1. Add a remote MCP server to Gemini CLI's configuration, with the address of Raphael's MCP server.
  2. When it connects, Gemini CLI opens the browser with Raphael's page: continue with Raphael's sign-in, below.

Raphael's sign-in#

It is the same for every assistant. The page comes in English; at the top right you can switch it to Spanish with EN | ES.

  1. Type your Raphael username and password and press Continue.
  2. The first time, a QR code appears. Scan it with your authenticator app and type the 6-digit code it shows. If you cannot scan, type into the app the key shown under the QR.
  3. Your 8 recovery codes appear. Keep them somewhere safe: each one works once if you lose your phone, and they are not shown again.
  4. The page tells you which assistant is asking for access, where it will return to, with which user and at which level. Check it is your assistant and press Allow.

Next times you only type your username, password and the app's code.

Check the page before pressing Allow

The page shows the domain your access will be sent to and whether Raphael recognises it ("Assistant recognised by Raphael", "An application on this same computer" or "Approved by your Raphael administrator"). If you do not recognise the assistant or the domain, press Cancel: whoever receives that access can read your company's data, and at the Detail level the patients'.

What you can ask#

Whatever you want about your operation. Some examples:

  • "How many hours did each driver work last week?"
  • "Which driver made the most trips in October?"
  • "Which vehicle drove the most miles this month?"
  • "Give me the cancelled trips by funding source, in a table."
  • "Compare this month's pickup punctuality with last month's."

Ask for any format: table, summary, list. To check the connector is on, ask it "which Raphael tools do you have?": it should name list_datasets, describe_dataset and run_query.

What you ask leaves Raphael

What your assistant queries is sent to the company behind that AI and stays in your conversation. At the Summary level the patients' name, address, phone, date of birth and e-mail do not travel. At the Detail level they do, which is why it is only granted when your company has a signed BAA with that vendor. Do not paste patient data from elsewhere into the conversation to complete an answer.

What to know about the answers#

  • Read-only. The assistant cannot change anything in Raphael.
  • You see what you see in the application. Never another company's data.
  • Hours and miles come from each route's daily record, which exists since vehicle inspection was switched on. There is no data before that date.
  • For past dates, a trip's driver may not be exact: the trip shows who has the route assigned today. The assistant will warn you when it matters.
  • Very large queries are cut. If the assistant says the result is incomplete, ask it to group it or narrow the dates.
  • Check the important figures. An assistant can misread a question. For a pay or staffing decision, compare with Raphael's report.

What can go wrong#

  • "Your user has no access to the Raphael MCP". Ask Raphael's administrator for it.
  • Your assistant says the domain "is not known yet" or that it could not register. You use a web assistant outside the list of four. Ask Raphael's administrator to approve it, or use one from the list.
  • Opening the address in the browser shows "This page isn't working". That is normal: the address is for your assistant, not for the browser.
  • "Too many attempts". Wait a few minutes: after several errors in a row access is locked for a while.
  • The app's code does not work. Wait for the next code; each one works only once. Check the phone's time is set automatically.
  • You lost your phone. Use a recovery code instead of the app's code. If you have none, ask Raphael's administrator to grant your access again: you will then enrol a new app.
  • "The time to enrol one is over". You have 7 days to enrol your app from when access is granted. Ask Raphael's administrator to grant it again.
  • The assistant stopped answering or asks you to connect again. Your access may have been taken away, your level changed (you then enrol the app again) or your session expired. Connect again from your assistant.
