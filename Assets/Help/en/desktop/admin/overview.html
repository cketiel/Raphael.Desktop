Integrators and notifications#

An integrator receives notices only when the status changes on a trip they created. They do not receive Will Calls or pickup arrivals: there is nothing they can do with that. See The notices, one by one.

Notification administration is somewhere else#

The channel panel, the rules, the kept records and the audit trail are not here: they open from the Notification Centre, with "Notification centre administration". See Notification administration.
