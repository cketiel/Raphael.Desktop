Admin — Billing

Billing items: what they are, their unit, their kind and how the charge formula uses them.

reference

Admin's Billing tab keeps the billing items: the things that can be charged (Miles, Loading Fee, Cancelation Fee, Holiday Fee, Wait Time…). A billing item has no price: each funding source sets the price in its rates.

What a billing item has#

FieldWhat it is
DescriptionThe name shown in rates, on the trip and in reports.
UnitWhat is counted: MILE (the trip's miles), UNIT (once per trip), HOUR (hours, of waiting for instance) or PERCENT (a percentage of the trip).
KindWhat it is for in the charge formula (see below).
Is Copay and the AR / AP accountsAccounting data for billing exports. They do not change the price.

The kind: how the formula uses it#

The charge formula reads the kind, never the name. Whether the item is called "MILES", "Mileage" or "Millas" does not matter: if its kind is Miles, it is multiplied by the trip's miles.

KindHow it is chargedUsual unit
Loading feeOnce per trip: the base fee.UNIT
MilesFor every mile of the trip, with free miles and bands if the rate has them.MILE
CancellationOnly when the trip is cancelled, as a percentage of what it would have cost. It must be PERCENT.PERCENT
AdditionalOnly when added to the trip (by hand or to a whole day).UNIT, HOUR

⚠️ The kind and unit of a billing item that already has rates cannot be changed: they would change the price of trips already billed. If it was a mistake, create a new billing item.

The list#

  • Sorted by importance: Miles, Loading Fee and Cancelation Fee first, then the rest.
  • The Kind column says what each is for, and In use in how many funding sources price it.
  • A billing item in use cannot be deleted.

Billing items by owner#

The list is grouped by who maintains each item:

  • First the Office: the items the office defines and that can be priced in any funding source.
  • Then one group per clinic, with its name. The clinic creates those items from the Booking Portal's Admin tab and prices them in its own funding source.

Each group can be folded. The office can open and correct a clinic's item: a notice at the top of the form says whose it is. The clinic sees the change, and the item stays theirs.

When you add a rate to a funding source, the Billing item list offers the office's items and those of the clinics that use that funding source, as "Description — Clinic". One clinic's item never shows on another's funding source, so a clinic is never billed at another clinic's prices.

Adding a new item, step by step#

Example: an After Hours Fee, charged once on trips outside office hours.

  1. Admin → Billing → + (new).
  2. Description: After Hours Fee · Unit: UNIT · Kind: Additional.
  3. Save.
  4. Go to Funding Sources and create its rate in every funding source and space type that pays for it (for instance, $45 on WCH), without ticking Default. See Funding source rates.
  5. From then on it shows in the Billing section of those funding sources' trips, to add with +.
