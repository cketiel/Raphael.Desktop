Admin — Funding source rates

What a rate is, how to set one up step by step, how a trip is priced, the simulator, copy, renew, retire and applying a charge to a day with undo.

reference

Every funding source (Medicaid, an insurer, a clinic…) pays for its trips according to its rates. This page explains what a rate is, how to set one up, how a trip's price is worked out from it, and every tool on the screen.

⚠️ This is a critical process. All of Raphael's money —the trip's Billing section in Home, the day's total, the Production, Facility and Manifest reports, the Booking Portal and the AI assistant (MCP)— comes from these rates and one single formula. A wrong rate is billed wrong everywhere at once; a right one, right everywhere.

What a rate is#

A rate answers one question: "how much does this funding source pay for this item, in this kind of vehicle, on these dates?". So every rate has four parts:

PartWhat it isExample
Funding sourceWho paysSAFERIDE
Billing itemWhat is charged, with its unitMiles (per mile), Loading Fee (per trip)
Space typeThe trip's space typeAMB (ambulatory), WCH (wheelchair), STR (stretcher)
ValidityFrom which day to which day it applies01/01/2026 → 12/31/2026

And an amount: $2.25 per mile, $31 per trip…

The same item is worth different amounts per space type. A stretcher trip (STR) costs more than an ambulatory one (AMB), which is why each space type has its own rates.

Billing item kinds#

Raphael does not read an item's name to know what to do with it, but its kind (set up in Billing):

KindWhat it is forExamples
Loading feeThe base fee of each trip, once per tripLoading Fee
MilesMultiplied by the trip's milesMiles
CancellationOnly applies when the trip is cancelledCancelation Fee
AdditionalCharges added when neededHoliday Fee, Weekend Fee, After Hours Fee, Wait Time, Bariatric Fee

Default and additional#

This is the most important distinction on the screen:

  • A default rate applies by itself to every trip of that funding source and space type. Nobody has to remember to add it. Normally the Loading Fee and the Miles are default.
  • An additional rate does not apply by itself: it is added to a trip when someone picks it in the trip's Billing section, or to all of a day's trips with the calendar button (below). Example: a Holiday Fee is only charged on a holiday's trips.
  • The Cancelation Fee is neither: it only comes into play when the trip is cancelled.

⚠️ A trip can only receive the additional rates set up for its funding source and space type. If a funding source pays a Holiday Fee on WCH but nobody set it up, the trip will not offer it. Set up every one the contract covers.

The screen at a glance#

Admin → Billing → Funding Sources.

On the left, the funding sources.

  • Each with its initials and, underneath, "N rates in force" (or No rates in force).
  • Search funding source filters by name and Active only hides inactive ones.
  • An orange triangle warns of a problem: no rates, or a space type missing its Loading Fee or Miles in force today. Hover over it to read which.
  • The funding source you pick is remembered when you switch tabs and come back.

Top right, the selected funding source's header, with four figures:

  • Rates in force: how many apply today.
  • Space types: in how many space types it has rates.
  • Ending in 6 months: default rates expiring soon (see below).
  • Warnings: setup problems.

And the buttons Details (edit the funding source), Copy rates from… and + Rate.

Below, one card per space type (AMB, STR, WCH…):

  • The header shows the space type, how many default and additional rates it has, and its warnings. Click the header to fold or unfold the card: the arrow on the left shows which. Cards you fold stay folded while the application is open, also when the screen updates itself.
  • + Rate on the header opens a new rate with that space type already chosen.
  • Rates always in the same order: Loading Fee, Miles, Cancelation Fee, then the additional ones.
  • Each row: the kind's icon, the name and a tag (Default, Additional, Future if it starts later, Expired), the amount, the conditions in words ("7 miles free", "max $50", "% of the trip", "not charged"), the validity and whether it has priced trips ("priced trips until 11/18/2026" or "never used").
  • On the right of each row: Edit (pencil), Retire or delete (archive box) and, on additional rates, the calendar to apply it to a day.
  • At the foot, the simulator.

Show history (expired rates) also shows rates that have expired. It starts unticked. Bottom left, Ending soon (N) opens the list of rates to renew.

The screen updates itself: if another dispatcher, or a clinic from the Booking Portal, creates, changes or retires a rate, you see it straight away. And when you change the language, it changes at once.

Setting up a new funding source, step by step#

Example: SAFERIDE pays $31 per wheelchair trip (WCH), $2.25 a mile with the first 5 free, $40 on holidays, and does not charge cancellations.

  1. Pick SAFERIDE on the left. With no rates you see "This funding source has no rates": its trips are worth $0, with a warning, until you set them up.
  2. Click + Rate (or + Rate on the WCH card if it already exists).
  3. The Loading Fee:
    • Billing item: Loading Fee · Space type: WCH.
    • Rate: 31 · tick Default.
    • From 01/01/2026 · To 12/31/2026.
    • Save.
  4. The miles:
    • Billing item: Miles · Space type: WCH.
    • Rate: 2.25 · tick Default.
    • Free quantity: 5 (the first 5 miles are not charged).
    • Same dates · Save.
  5. The cancellation:
    • Billing item: Cancelation Fee (its unit is PERCENT) · Space type: WCH.
    • Rate: 0 → a cancelled trip costs 0 % of what it would have cost: not charged.
    • Not Default (Raphael does not allow it).
    • Same dates · Save.
  6. The holiday:
    • Billing item: Holiday Fee · Space type: WCH · Rate: 40.
    • Not Default: it is additional.
    • Same dates · Save.
  7. Repeat for every space type it pays for (AMB, STR…), or use Copy rates from… if another funding source has similar prices.
  8. Check with the simulator at the foot of the card: 12 miles → $31 + 7 × $2.25 = $46.75.

The form warns before saving about anything the server would refuse: an empty required field, dates the wrong way round, Free quantity and min qty together, a percentage outside 0–100… So no time is lost on a mistake that could be seen.

Every field of the form#

Price

FieldWhat it means
Billing itemThe item. Its unit (MILE, UNIT, HOUR, PERCENT) says what is multiplied.
Space typeWhich space type it is for.
RateThe amount per unit. With the PERCENT unit, a percentage (0 to 100).
Per (units)The rate is per that many units. Empty = 1. Example: $30 "per" 60 minutes.
Procedure codeThe billing code (HCPCS) printed on reports. It does not change the price.
Min chargeThe line never costs less. Example: miles at $2 with a $10 minimum → a 3-mile trip bills $10.
Max chargeThe line never costs more. Example: Wait Time capped at $90.
DefaultApplies by itself to every trip (see above). Otherwise it is additional.

Quantities

FieldWhat it means
Free quantityThe first units are not charged. With 7 free miles, a 15.3-mile trip bills 8.3.
Greater than (>) min qtyThe same said another way: only what goes beyond it is charged.
Less or equal (<=) max qtyHow far this rate reaches. Used to build bands.

⚠️ Free quantity and Greater than min qty are the same lower edge: fill in only one. Typing in one locks the other.

Bands, like tax brackets. If the contract says "the first 10 miles at $3 and from there on at $2.50", you create two Miles rates:

  • One with Less or equal max qty = 10 and rate 3.
  • Another with Greater than min qty = 10 and rate 2.50.

A 15-mile trip bills 10 × $3 + 5 × $2.50 = $42.50. Each rate bills only its own band, never the whole trip.

Validity

FieldWhat it means
From / ToThe days it applies, both included. Only the day counts, never the time.

How a trip is priced#

Always the same way, on the server:

  1. It takes the rates of the trip's funding source and space type in force on the trip's date (not today). A report from October to November prices each trip with its own day's rate.
  2. It adds up the default rates: the Loading Fee once and the Miles for the trip's miles, with free miles, bands, "per", minimums and maximums.
  3. A PERCENT rate is a percentage of what the trip's default rates add up to.
  4. It adds the additional rates added to the trip.
  5. If the trip is cancelled, instead of all the above only its Cancelation Fee is charged: a percentage of what it would have cost. At 0.00 %, a cancelled trip is not charged. If the funding source has no Cancelation Fee, the trip is $0 and flagged with a warning; Raphael never assumes a percentage.

A complete example (SAFERIDE, STR: Loading Fee $120, Miles $4.00 with 7 free):

TripWorkingTotal
6 miles$120 + (6 − 7 → 0 miles)$120.00
15.3 miles$120 + 8.3 × $4.00 = $120 + $33.20$153.20
15.3 miles, holiday$153.20 + Holiday Fee $40$193.20
15.3 miles, cancelled0 % of $153.20$0.00

When the price is fixed. Every trip keeps its charge, line by line. While it is open it is recalculated by itself whenever something that affects it changes (miles, funding source, space type, date, a rate, an additional charge). Once it finishes or is cancelled, it is frozen: a later rate change no longer touches it, and reports show it as it closed.

The simulator#

At the foot of every card: Simulate a trip of [__] miles → $XX.XX, and on the right cancelled → $X.XX.

  • Type some miles and you see straight away what a trip of that space type would cost today, with the line-by-line breakdown underneath.
  • On the right, what it would cost if it were cancelled (with the cancellation rate).
  • The server works it out with the same formula as trips and reports. If the simulator gives an odd figure, the rate is set up wrong: fix it before it is billed.

Use it every time you create or change rates: with miles below the free ones, above them, and at the edge of every band.

Editing a rate: history cannot be rewritten#

A rate that has not priced any trip yet can be edited freely.

A rate that has priced trips is history. If its amount could change, trips already billed would change price after being invoiced. So:

  • When you open it, a box at the top of the form gives the last day it priced a trip and explains what will happen.
  • Its amount, quantities, item, space type and start date are not rewritten. If you change the amount and save, Raphael creates a new period: the current rate ends the day before New period from and the new one starts that day with the new values. It asks you to confirm, giving both dates.
  • Its end date and procedure code can be changed in place. The end date never before the last trip it priced.

Example: WCH's Holiday Fee goes from $40 to $45 on December 1. Edit the rate, type 45, set New period from to 12/01/2026 and save. Result: $40 until 11/30 and $45 from 12/01. November's trips still cost what they cost. With Show history you see both periods.

Open trips affected by the change are recalculated by themselves within seconds.

Retire or delete#

The archive box on each row:

  • If the rate has priced trips, it is retired: it stops applying from today and stays in the history (tick Show history to see it). It cannot be deleted.
  • If it never priced a trip, it is deleted for good.

You are always asked to confirm, and the message says which of the two will happen.

What Raphael will not save#

The server enforces this, not only this screen: it cannot be bypassed from the Booking Portal or any other application either.

  • Two overlapping rates: same funding source, space type and billing item (or two different Loading Fees), with dates and bands that overlap. They would bill the same thing twice. Change the dates or the bands, or edit the one that exists.
  • A rate that ends before it starts.
  • Free quantity and Greater than min qty together.
  • A max qty not above the free or minimum quantity: it would never bill anything.
  • A max charge below the minimum.
  • A Cancelation Fee that is not PERCENT, or marked Default.
  • A percentage outside 0–100.

Copying rates from another funding source#

To set up a funding source with prices like another's without typing them one by one.

  1. On the left, pick the funding source that will receive the rates.
  2. Click Copy rates from….
  3. In Copy from, pick the funding source to copy from.
  4. In The copies start on, the date they will apply from. The rates the other one has in force on that day are copied.
  5. Click Copy.
  • Each copy starts on that date and ends when the original ended.
  • If the funding source already has a rate in the same place (same item and space type, overlapping), that one is skipped, never overwritten. When it finishes, Raphael says how many were copied and which were skipped and why.
  • Then review and adjust the amounts that differ (with the simulator).

Rates ending soon, and renewing#

Ending soon (N), bottom left, lists the default rates of active funding sources that end in the next 6 months and have no period after them. These are the ones that, if nobody acts, would leave trips at $0.

  • Renew creates the next period with the same values, starting the day after it ends and with the same length. It asks you to confirm, with the dates.
  • If the new price is different, renew and then edit the new period (it has priced nothing yet, so it can be edited freely).
  • Once renewed, the rate leaves the list.
  • Back returns to the cards.

Applying an additional rate to all of a day's trips#

For the usual case: 300 trips are imported and only afterwards someone notices it was a holiday. Instead of opening the trips one by one, the charge is added to all of them at once.

  1. On the space type's card, click the calendar of the additional rate (for instance, STR's Holiday Fee). It only shows on additional rates in force.
  2. Choose the Day and Add (or Remove).
  3. Before changing anything, Raphael tells you how many trips will change and how much money it adds up to, how many already had it and how many are finished or cancelled (those do not change).
  4. Click Apply and confirm.

Rules:

  • Only the open trips of that funding source and that space type on that day change. If the holiday affects WCH and STR, do it on both cards.
  • A trip that already has it does not get it twice.
  • Finished and cancelled trips keep their charge.
  • Totals update within seconds, in Home and in the reports.

If it was a mistake: Undo#

After Apply, the dialog stays open and an orange box appears: "Last applied to N trip(s) of this day ($X) on … by …. Was it a mistake?" with the Undo button.

  • Undo takes the charge off only the trips that got it in that application. A trip someone had added it to by hand before keeps it.
  • If you close the dialog and notice later, open the calendar of that rate again, choose the same day and the box is still there.
  • Remove is different: it takes the charge off every open trip of the day that has it, also those that got it by hand. To fix a mistake, use Undo.

Past days are protected as everywhere in Raphael: if the day has passed, the server refuses the change and says why. To correct a trip of a past day, open it in Home as an administrator and type the reason for the correction.

Frequently asked questions#

A trip shows $0. Why?#

Open the trip and look at the Billing section: an orange warning gives the reason. The usual ones: the funding source has no default rates in force on the trip's date for that space type, the trip is cancelled (and cancellation is at 0 %), or it has no funding source.

A trip does not show the Miles line#

It is not missing: it shows at $0.00 with a blue explanation, for instance "6 mi trip, within the first 7 mi free · not billed". When there are miles to bill, the Miles line says how many were free and how many are billed.

I changed a rate and last week's trips did not change#

That is right if they were finished or cancelled: their charge froze when they closed. Open trips are recalculated.

Why can't I change a rate's amount?#

Because it has priced trips. Change the amount and save: a new period is created from the date you choose, without touching what was already billed.
