Admin — Funding source rates

How each funding source's rates are set up and priced, and why their history cannot be rewritten.

referenceNew in 1.20.0

Each funding source pays for its trips according to its rates. A rate says how much is charged for a billing item (Miles, Loading Fee, Cancelation Fee, Holiday Fee…) for a space type (AMB, WCH, WCX, BWCH, STR) during a period of dates.

⚠️ This is a critical process. All the money in the system — the production report, the Manifest, the Facility report, the Home total, Booking and the AI assistant (MCP) — comes from these rates and from one single formula. A wrong rate is charged wrong everywhere at once.

The screen#

  • On the left, the funding sources, with a search box and the Active only filter. An orange triangle flags a configuration problem: no rates, or a space type without a Loading Fee or Miles in force today. Hover over it to read it. The chosen funding source is remembered when you switch tabs.
  • On the right, its rates, one card per space type. Every card follows the same order: Loading Fee, Miles, Cancelation Fee, then the additional ones.
  • Each rate has a label: Default, Additional, Future (starts later) or Expired. Under its validity you can read whether it has priced trips and until when.
  • Show history also lists expired rates. It starts unticked.

Default and additional#

  • A default rate is added by itself to every trip of that funding source and space type. Usually: Loading Fee and Miles.
  • An additional rate (Holiday Fee, Weekend fee, After Hours Fee, Stretcher Wait Time…) is added to a trip only when someone chooses it. Only the ones set up on the trip's funding source can be added, so set them all up.
  • The Cancelation Fee is never default: it applies only when the trip is cancelled.

How a trip is priced#

  1. The rates in force on the trip's date are used, not today's. A report from October to November prices each trip with its own month's rate.
  2. The default rates and the chosen additional ones are added up.
  3. Each field of a rate counts only when it is filled in:
FieldWhat it does
Free quantityThose units are not charged. With 7 free miles, a 12-mile trip charges 5.
Greater than (>) min qtySame as free quantity. Fill in only one of them: the form locks the other.
Less or equal (<=) max qtyCloses the band. Several rates of the same item make tiers, like tax brackets: "up to 10 miles at $3" and "above 10 at $2.50" price a 15-mile trip as 10 × $3 + 5 × $2.50.
Per (units)The rate is per that many units. Empty means 1.
Min / max chargeThe line's amount never goes below the minimum or above the maximum.
PERCENT unitThe rate is a percentage of what the trip's default rates cost.
  1. A cancelled trip is charged only its Cancelation Fee, a percentage of what it would have cost. Today they are all 0.00 %: cancelled trips are not charged. If a funding source has no Cancelation Fee, the cancelled trip is $0 and flagged; no percentage is ever assumed.

The simulator#

At the foot of each card, type some miles and you see at once what that trip would cost today, line by line, and what it would cost cancelled. The server works it out with the same formula as the reports, so if the simulator shows an odd figure, the rate is wrong.

History cannot be rewritten#

A rate that has priced trips is history: changing its amount would change what those trips cost after they were billed. So:

  • Its amount, quantities, item, space type and start date cannot change. If you change the amounts and save, Raphael creates a new period from the date you choose and ends the current one the day before. The form explains this at the top before saving.
  • Its end date and its procedure code can change, but the end date never before the last trip it priced.
  • Retire (archive icon) does not erase it: it ends it yesterday and keeps it in the history. Only a rate that never priced a trip is really deleted. You are always asked first.

The server enforces all of this, not just this screen: no other application can get around it.

What Raphael will not save#

  • Two rates of the same item, funding source and space type that overlap in dates and bands. They would charge the same units twice. Change the dates or the bands, or edit the existing one.
  • A rate that ends before it begins.
  • Free quantity and Greater than min qty together.
  • A max qty not above the free or min quantity: it would never charge.
  • A Cancelation Fee that is not PERCENT, or that is default.
  • A percentage outside 0–100.

Copy rates from another funding source#

Copy rates from… copies to the chosen funding source the rates of another one in force on a date. The copies start that day and end when the original ended. A rate it already has in the same place is skipped and reported, never overwritten. Handy for setting up similar clinics.

Rates ending soon#

The Ending soon button lists the default rates of active funding sources that end in the next 6 months and have no period after them. Renew creates the next period with the same values, starting the day after it ends; if the price changes, edit the new period afterwards. Once renewed, the rate leaves the list.

Apply an additional rate to a day's trips#

Additional rates still in force have a calendar button. It is for the day 300 trips were imported and someone notices it is a holiday: instead of editing the trips one by one, the charge is added to all of them at once.

  1. Press the calendar on the rate's line (for example, WCH's Holiday Fee).
  2. Choose the day and whether to add or remove the charge.
  3. Raphael tells you before doing anything how many trips will change and how much money that is, how many already had it and how many are finished or cancelled.
  4. Press Apply and confirm.
  • Only open trips of that funding source and that space type change (the rate is per space type). Finished and cancelled trips keep their charge.
  • A trip that already has it does not get it twice.
  • Remove takes the fee off every open trip of the day that has it, also those that got it by hand.
  • Undo: if it was a mistake, open the calendar of that rate and day again. The orange box says when and by whom it was last applied, and to how many trips; Undo takes it off those trips only. Trips that had it added by hand keep it. After applying, the dialog stays open with the box ready, in case it was a mistake.
  • The screen updates by itself when someone changes rates, and the language changes at once.
  • Totals update within a few seconds.
