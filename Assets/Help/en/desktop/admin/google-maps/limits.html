Google Maps: the limit of the suggestions

How much the suggestions can cost in a month, what each setting means and an example with 300 trips a day.

how-toNew in 1.23.15

The routing suggestions compare every line for free. Only the first options of each list are asked to Google, which charges each new road (the way from one point to another). Here you decide how many roads a day may be bought, and so how much can be spent in a month.

What you see first#

At the top, large: with these settings, at most per month. It is the limit times the days of the month, at Google's price. It cannot go beyond that figure.

What each setting means#

SettingWhat it is
Options of each list asked to GoogleThe first N of each list carry the real drive; the rest, "estimated"
Limit: roads a day for suggestionsHow many roads a day may be bought for that, shared by everyone
Appointment checks: roads a dayThe same, to confirm impossible and loose trips

With 3 options, the list of a trip buys up to 7 roads (1 of the patient + 2 per line) and the list of a line, up to 9 (3 per trip).

Example: 300 trips and 30 lines a day#

It is today's production volume. With 3 options and opening the suggestions once in every trip and every line:

  1. Trips: 300 × 7 = 2,100 roads.
  2. Lines: 30 × 9 = 270 roads.
  3. Maximum: 2,370 roads a day × 30 days = 71,100 a month.
  4. Realistic: taking off the 30% cancelled, 1,659 a day, 49,770 a month.

The screen turns those roads into dollars with the stored prices. Opening the same suggestions again costs nothing: the roads bought are stored on the server.

Steps#

  1. In Admin, Google Maps, Settings, scroll to Google daily limit for the suggestions.
  2. Change the example (trips, lines, cancelled, days) to your case. None of it is saved.
  3. Limit for the realistic case sets the limit that covers the realistic day. Back to the start undoes.
  4. Press Save settings. It applies in less than a minute.

A limit turns nothing off

When it runs out, the suggestions go on and say "estimated" until the next day. With 0 nothing is bought.

What can go wrong#

It says it does not reach the realistic day. Raise the limit or ask fewer options: the table compares 1 to 6.

Google's invoice does not match. These are estimates with the prices stored in the panel.
