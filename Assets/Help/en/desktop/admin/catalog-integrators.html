Catalog — Integrators

The directory of residences, hospitals and care centres we do not work with yet, and how to move one into your company.

how-toNew in 1.11.0

The Integrators tab in Admin holds the entities that already send trips into the system. This catalog holds the others: the residences, hospitals and care centres there is no arrangement with yet, the ones somebody has to find, call and convince.

A catalog entity is never deleted. When it stops being of interest it is marked retired and stays where it is: the reason somebody looked at it once outlives today's lack of interest.

The three groups#

GroupWhat it holds
Nursing homesCMS-certified facilities
Assisted living / residential careALF, AFCH, ICF/DD and APD homes
HospitalsIncluding hospital units and associated sites

An entity is never deleted, even when it stops being of interest

The expensive thing in this catalog is not the name: it is the phone number somebody verified, the person they managed to reach and what that person said. Deleting a row because the approach went nowhere throws all of that away, and a year later somebody starts again from nothing with the same residence. That is why retiring one keeps it reachable through Include retired, and why moving one into your company does not take it out of here either.

Searching#

The search box takes several words at once and requires all of them, even when they sit in different fields: typing a name and a city together finds the row that holds one in its name and the other in its city.

Three things worth knowing:

  • Accents do not matter. Typing gonzalez finds González.
  • Neither does phone formatting. Type digits only and the number is searched, so all three shapes a phone takes in the source files reach the same row.
  • Results are ordered by relevance, not alphabetically. Entities whose name begins with what you typed come first.

When nothing matches, similar names appear under the box. That is the answer to a typo: a misspelled word finds nothing, but offers the names that start the same way.

The group and county dropdowns, and the In my company and Include retired boxes, combine with whatever you typed. The funnel button puts everything back.

Adding an entity to your company#

The building icon opens the Integrator form already filled in from what the catalog knows. It creates nothing on its own, and that is deliberate: an integrator is born with a live API key against our system, and that should not happen behind anybody's back.

Check the details, fill in what is missing and save. Then:

  • The integrator appears on the Integrators tab, with its key.
  • The catalog row stays where it was, marked with a green seal.
  • That mark belongs to your company. If you work under the general broker, what they added does not show as yours and yours does not show as theirs. The same entity can be in several companies at once.

Importing a file#

The spreadsheet icon opens the import window.

  1. Choose the file.
  2. Choose the source format: there is one per group, and it is what says which column is which.
  3. Look at the preview. The line above it says how many rows came out, which row the header was on, and how many rows without a name were skipped — the source files carry plenty of blank rows and those are not a problem.
  4. If the format does not recognise a column in the file, you are told. That column is not imported.
  5. Press Import. It goes in batches, and you can stop it.

Running the same file again duplicates nothing. Each entity is recognised by its group, its name and its ZIP, so a second pass corrects the rows rather than repeating them. That is also why stopping halfway loses nothing: what was sent is saved, and the next pass finishes the rest.

Why fewer entities arrive than the file has rows#

Because the files name the same entity more than once, and say so themselves: the hospitals sheet prints "una institución puede aparecer en más de un registro" above its own header. One hospital appears once as Acute Care Hospitals and again as PART A PROVIDER — HOSPITAL: two CMS records of the same building, and for the purpose of phoning somebody that is one place, not two.

Those rows are merged into a single entity, and the Detail column on the right says which. The 381 rows of the hospitals file are 331 entities and 50 merged rows.

Merging loses nothing: if one record carries the email and the other does not, the entity keeps the email. An empty cell in these files means "not verified", not "has none" — each sheet says so in its own notes — so a gap never erases what another row did know.

Coordinates#

Entities arrive without coordinates, and they are fetched only when needed, with the marker button, for the rows on the page that have none.

⚠️ The whole system shares a cap of 1,500 geocoding lookups a day. That is why they are not fetched during an import: a catalog of thousands of rows would burn several days of quota and leave tomorrow's trips without coordinates.

Who changed what#

Opening an entity shows a line at the bottom: where the row came from, who last changed it, from which company, and when. It is a record, not a field — it cannot be edited.
