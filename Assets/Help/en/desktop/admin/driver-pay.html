A driver's pay rate — hourly or per mile

How to see and change what each driver is paid, per paid hour or per mile with a patient, from which date, and how to read their history.

how-toNew in 1.22.0

Every driver has their own pay rate. There are two options and only one applies at a time:

OptionWhat is paid
HourlyEvery hour of the paid shift in the productivity report.
Per mileEvery mile driven with a patient on board. With two patients at once, that mile counts twice.

To begin with every driver is hourly, at $15.00, with $1.00 per mile kept in case they switch.

Open the pay window#

  1. Open Admin → Employees → Users and the Drivers tab.
  2. The Pay rate column shows what each driver is paid today: blue with a clock for hourly, purple with a road for per mile, and orange No rate when there is none.
  3. Click that rate, or open the row's ⋮ menu and choose Pay rate.

What the window shows#

  • The purple band at the top: the driver and what they are paid today, with the option and since when.
  • 1 · How the driver is paid: two cards, Hourly and Per mile, each with its amount.
  • 2 · From when: the date the new rate starts and an optional note.
  • The blue box says in one sentence what saving will do.
  • Show rate history: the earlier rates, only when you ask.
  • At the bottom: Cancel and Save rate. Press F1 at any time to open this help.

Change the rate#

  1. Mark the card of the option the driver will be paid by. The marked card has the filled circle, the purple border and the label This is the one paid. The other says Click to pay this way and looks faded.
  2. Type the amount. Clicking a card's amount marks that card: you cannot type in one and leave the other marked without noticing.
  3. Change the other card's amount too if you like. Both are saved: the unmarked one is kept in reserve in case the driver switches later.
  4. Choose Effective from: the first day paid at the new rate. It can be in the past (to correct) or in the future (to schedule it).
  5. Read the blue box and click Save rate.

The Pay rate column in the list updates at once.

The history#

Click Show rate history. The 10 most recent appear, newest first, each with its date, option, amount, the amount kept in reserve, the note, who set it and when. The one that applies today is marked In force, and one with a future date Scheduled. If there are more, click Load 10 more. The list has a fixed height and scrolls inside, so the window never grows taller than the screen.

Why a rate has a date

A rate change does not rewrite the past. If a driver switches to per mile on the 15th, the productivity report pays the 1st to the 14th hourly and the 15th onwards per mile. A month that has already been paid always gives the same total, however late you run the report again.

If you make a mistake

Save again with the same date: the new rate replaces that day's one.

Administrators only

Pay rates are payroll data. Only an administrator sees the Pay rate column, the pay window and the amounts in the productivity report. Each Provider sees only its own drivers.
