Schedules — planning the day

The open trips grid and how trips are assigned to routes, plus the revenue and graph views.

referenceNew in 1.3.0

Schedules is where a trip goes from existing to having a vehicle behind it.

Open Schedules

SectionWhat it is
ScheduleThe day's planning: the open trips and the routes they are assigned to
RevenueThe day in money
GraphsThe day in charts

Two things that happen here and show up in notifications#

Turning a trip into a Will Call, and undoing it. Both are done from the open trips grid, and both produce their own notice so the office finds out. See Will Call.

The grid reacts to cancellations. If you have the grid in front of you and a trip in it is cancelled, the row is marked cancelled, the grid brings it into view, and a few seconds later removes it. It is there so nobody schedules a trip that no longer exists.
