Why the screen changes on its own

What feeds each zone of Schedule, what announces itself and what does not, and what happens when the connection drops.

whyNew in 1.6.0

The Schedule tab is not a photograph of the day: it is the day. Several dispatchers work it at once, drivers report from the road, and whatever any of them does shows up on your screen without you reloading anything.

Do not look for a refresh button. There is none because there is no need for one.

Each zone of the screen is fed by different things, and knowing which is what tells you what to expect from it.

The unrouted trips list#

This is the whole day, so here you see what everyone is doing, whichever route they are working on.

What happensWhat you see
Another dispatcher routes a tripThe row leaves your list, quietly
Another dispatcher takes a trip off a routeIt comes back
A trip in front of you is cancelledThe row is marked red, brought into view, and leaves on its own
Somebody turns a trip into a Will CallIts hours change and its button becomes "activate"
Somebody activates a Will CallIt takes the agreed hour and its button goes back to "make Will Call"

The last two matter more than they look. The button you see is the one that matches the trip's state. If that row never heard about the change, the screen would go on offering "activate" on a trip that is no longer waiting for anybody, or let you read an hour that was rewritten ten minutes ago.

The route grid#

This zone shows one route: the one you picked above. It only hears about that one.

What happensWhat you see
The driver reaches a stopIts flag turns green
The driver performs a stopIts flag turns purple
Somebody reorders this routeIt reloads with the new order and the new ETAs
Somebody adds or removes a trip on this routeThe same: it reloads whole

The flags come from the driver's app, not from the office: it is the driver who presses "arrived" and "performed" out on the road, and their stop changes colour on your screen the moment they do. That used to appear only on a reload, and a dispatcher could watch a route for minutes without seeing that it had already moved.

When somebody reorders, the grid does not just change the order: it reloads whole, because moving one stop moves the hours of every stop behind it.

If somebody reorders a route you do not have open, you will see nothing

And that is correct. That grid is the route you chose. If you want to see it, choose it.

The map#

The vehicle's arrow advances with the driver's reports, one every 30 seconds, animated between one and the next. The details are in Schedules — planning the day.

Why some things announce themselves and others do not#

The bell only rings for something you have to know: a cancellation is news, because it changes what has to happen to that patient. An activated Will Call is too — somebody is waiting to be given an hour.

A colleague taking a trip is not. If every one of a day's four hundred trips interrupted everybody the moment it was routed, the bell would stop meaning anything — and with it the cancellations, which are the ones that matter.

So a trip routed by somebody else disappears without a sound. It is not that you missed it: there was nothing to miss.

It never changes under your hands#

While you are dragging a stop, routing, or saving something, the screen does not reload. It waits until you finish. If anything changed meanwhile, you will see it when you are done.

That is deliberate: moving the ground under somebody mid-operation is worse than being two seconds out of date.

If the screen goes still#

The connection to the server can drop — the office network, or the server restarting. The screen does not sit there in a false state: it keeps working, it stops hearing what the others are doing, and it reconnects on its own as soon as it can.

Whatever was missed does not need recovering: change the date and come back, or change the route and come back, and the screen loads again from the server.

Still is not always broken

A route whose driver has not left the garage has nothing to report, and by mid-afternoon nobody may be routing. Before suspecting the connection, check whether anything is actually happening.

Before this, the screen refreshed itself every 5 seconds

Not any more. The server now says when something changes, which is less work for your machine and less waiting for you.
