Why the screen changes on its own

Several dispatchers work the same day; what updates live, what does not, and why not everything announces itself.

whyNew in 1.6.0

The Schedule tab is not a photograph of the day: it is the day. Several dispatchers work on it at once, and what one of them does appears on the others' screens without anybody reloading anything.

Do not look for a refresh button. There is none because there is no need for one.

What updates on its own#

What happensWhat you see
Another dispatcher routes a tripThe row leaves your unrouted list, quietly
Another dispatcher takes a trip off a routeIt reappears in your list
Somebody reorders the route you have openIt reloads with the new ETAs
The driver reaches a stopIts flag turns green
The driver performs a stopIts flag turns purple
A trip in front of you is cancelledThe row is marked, brought into view, and leaves
The vehicle movesThe arrow on the map advances

Why some things announce themselves and others do not#

The bell only rings for something you have to know: a cancellation is news, because it changes what has to happen to that patient.

A colleague taking a trip is not. If each of the four hundred trips of a day interrupted everybody when it was routed, the bell would stop meaning anything — and with it the cancellations, which are the ones that matter.

So a trip routed by somebody else disappears quietly. It is not that you missed it: there was nothing to be told.

It never changes under your hands#

While you are dragging a stop or saving something, the screen does not reload. It waits until you are done. If anything changed meanwhile, you see it when you finish.

That is deliberate: moving the ground under somebody mid-operation is worse than being two seconds out of date.

The top grid is one route#

If another dispatcher reorders a route you do not have open, you see nothing, and that is right: that grid shows the route you picked. To see it, pick that route.

The list below, in contrast, is the whole day, so there you do see what everybody is doing.

If the screen goes quiet#

The connection to the server can be lost — the office network, or the server restarting. The screen does not sit in a false state: it keeps working; it stops hearing what the others do, and it reconnects on its own as soon as it can.

What was missed does not have to be recovered: change the date and come back, or change the route and come back, and the screen loads again from the server.

Before this, the screen refreshed itself every 5 seconds

Not any more. The server now says when something changed, which is less work for your machine and less waiting for you.
