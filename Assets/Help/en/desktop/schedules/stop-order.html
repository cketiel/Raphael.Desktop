Change the order of the stops

Dragging a stop within a route, what gets recalculated, and what the system will not let you do.

how-toNew in 1.6.0

Changing the order of the stops changes the driver's actual round: the order in this grid is the order they will drive.

When you need it#

The system places each stop by its hour, and sometimes that is not the best round. Two pickups on the same street twenty minutes apart, a dialysis centre worth reaching before it fills up, a patient to drop off on the way instead of doubling back.

The steps#

  1. Pick the route, and check you are on the right date.
  2. In the top grid, drag the row to where you want it and drop it.

On drop, the system saves the new order and recalculates the ETAs of every stop from there down, including the hour the vehicle leaves the garage.

Moving one stop moves every stop behind it

ETAs are a chain: each stop inherits the previous one's hour, plus the time the vehicle spends there, plus the drive. Pulling a stop forward delays everything behind it, and every one of those hours is a patient waiting at a door.

After reordering, look for rows that have turned red: that is the screen telling you the new order breaks a promise that has already been made.

What the system will not let you do#

Cross the two stops of one trip. You cannot leave the dropoff before its pickup, or the pickup after its dropoff. While you drag, an invalid position shows in the cursor and dropping there does nothing.

Move Pull-out or Pull-in. They are the garage departure and return. They are always the first and last stop, and they do not drag.

What can go wrong#

"Error saving order". Nothing was saved: the order you see reverts to what it was as soon as the grid refreshes. Try again; if it keeps failing, there is no connection to the server.

It reorders itself while you watch. Another dispatcher did it, on the same route. See Why the screen changes on its own.
