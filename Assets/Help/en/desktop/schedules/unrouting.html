Take a trip off a route

Returning a trip to the unrouted list, and how that differs from cancelling it.

how-toNew in 1.6.0

Taking a trip off a route removes its vehicle and returns it to the unrouted list. The trip still exists: somebody has to carry it, it is just not decided who.

This is not cancelling

Taking off a route = the patient still needs the trip, and it goes back in the queue. Cancelling = the trip is not happening. If what happened is that the patient is no longer going, do not take it off the route: cancel it, from the cancel button in the trip list.

When you need it#

The vehicle has broken down, the driver has not come in, the route is full, or reshuffling the day puts that trip on another one.

The steps#

  1. Pick the route carrying the trip.
  2. In the top grid, find either of its two stops — the pickup or the dropoff.
  3. Press the remove from route button on that row.

Both stops disappear, the rest of the route is renumbered, and the trip returns to the unrouted list below.

The hole that is left is your decision

The driver may already be on the road with that schedule on their phone. When you take the trip off, their app is told and corrects what it has on screen.

But the system does not reoptimise the rest of the route on its own: pulling the later stops forward would mean arriving early at the doors of people who were given a different hour. Look at the ETAs left and decide for yourself whether anything needs moving.

What can go wrong#

The garage stops do not disappear. Even if you take every trip off, Pull-out and Pull-in stay. That is deliberate: without them, the driver's app loses the ends of its working day.

It reappears below without you doing it. Another dispatcher took it off. See Why the screen changes on its own.
