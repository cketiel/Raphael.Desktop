Impossible and loose trips when importing

Which trips bring a pickup hour that does not fit their appointment, and how to adjust it when importing.

how-toNew in 1.23.15

When a file has been imported, Raphael checks the hours of every trip with an appointment and asks whether to adjust the ones that do not fit. Nothing changes until you press a button.

The two cases#

CaseWhat happensWhat the adjustment does
ImpossibleEven at the pickup on the hour, the appointment is missedMoves the pickup earlier
LooseMore than 30 minutes to spare: the patient would be there long beforeMoves the pickup later

If the hours contradict each other (the appointment is not after the pickup), there is no hour to offer: ask the broker.

Steps#

  1. Import the file as usual. When it ends, Pickup hours of the file opens with how many there are of each case and their share of the import.
  2. Choose: Adjust all, Adjust the impossible, Adjust the loose, or Adjust row by row. Undo puts the broker's hour back.
  3. Press Leave the rest (or close the window): what you did not adjust keeps the broker's hour.

If a trip is picked up 30 minutes or more earlier than the broker said, the office gets a notice to call the patient.

Importing again#

The file puts the broker's hours back and the window asks again. If it was adjusted before and not now, the old adjustment is gone; if not before and now yes, it is adjusted.

How it is worked out#

Time needed: boarding + drive (boarding from Space types: AMB 3, WCH 5, STR 10 min). The adjusted hour reaches the appointment about 15 minutes early.

The broker's hour is never lost

It is always kept. The report shows the original hour, the adjusted one, who and when, so it can be claimed.

What can go wrong#

A trip is missing. Returns without an appointment, Will Calls and trips already routed are not adjusted.
