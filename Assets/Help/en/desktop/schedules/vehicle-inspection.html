Vehicle inspection

What the driver inspects at Pull-out and Pull-in, where to see their answers and photos, and what tells the office.

referenceNew in 1.15.0

When the Provider has the vehicle inspection switched on, the driver inspects the vehicle in the Driver app at the Pull-out and at the Pull-in. Until it is finished they cannot Perform that event: neither set off on the route nor close the day.

What the driver answers#

  • The odometer, in miles. It is the reading of the Pull-out or the Pull-in: the same Odometer column as always, and the one the day's miles are worked out from.
  • The VIN: the app shows the VIN the system has and the driver confirms it is that vehicle. If it is another one, or the vehicle has no VIN on file, they photograph the VIN on the vehicle and type it.
  • The checklist, each line OK or Problem: body, lights, fluids, tires and fuel (above 80%), cleanliness and equipment. The wheelchair hooks are asked only of wheelchair vehicles. Every problem carries a photo taken with the camera.
  • A required comment and the driver's signature, which certifies that inspection. At Pull-in it replaces the end-of-shift signature.

Without coverage the inspection is kept on the phone and sent by itself when the connection comes back. The viewer then says when it arrived.

Seeing it from Schedule#

In the Schedule tab, the route's Pull-out and Pull-in rows have the inspection button. It opens that route and day's inspections: pick Pull-out or Pull-in at the top, the answers are on the left, and View photo shows a problem's photo on the right. The signature is below.

The photos and the signature are not saved on your computer: they are fetched from the server each time you open them.

Notices to the office#

If the inspection marks any problem, the office of the Provider the vehicle belongs to gets the notification Vehicle Inspection Problem, with the vehicle, the driver and how many lines. No problem stops the vehicle from leaving: it is a notice.

Switching it on#

Raphael's administrator switches it on or off for each Provider, in Admin → Providers. With it off, the driver works as always: the odometer and, at Pull-in, the signature.
