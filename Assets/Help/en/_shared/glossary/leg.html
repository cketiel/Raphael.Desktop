Leg — outbound and return

A round journey is not one trip, it is two: Appointment and Return, each with its own number and status.

reference

A round journey is not one trip. It is two, and the system treats them separately:

  • Appointment — the outbound leg: from home to the medical facility.
  • Return — the return leg: from the facility back home.

What it means that there are two#

Each leg is an independent record. It has its own trip number, its own status in the life cycle, its own assigned route and its own notices.

That is why, on an ordinary day, the same patient appears twice in the lists and produces two series of notices. It is not a duplicate.

When you are not sure

If you are unsure whether an action affects both legs, look in Dispatch: both appear separately, each with its number. What you see there is what is true.
