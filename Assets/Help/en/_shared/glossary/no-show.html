No-show

The vehicle reached the pickup point and the patient did not appear. The driver records it, always with a reason.

reference

A no-show is a trip where the vehicle reached the pickup point and the patient did not appear.

The driver records it from their application, and always with a reason. That reason travels with the trip and is visible in the office, in the "No-show reason" field of the trip details.

It is not a cancellation#

The difference matters, and it is not a matter of wording:

CancellationNo-show
When it happensBefore the vehicle arrivesWith the vehicle already at the door
Who records itThe office, the patient, the facility or the driverThe driver only
Operating costAvoidable if the notice comes in timeAlready incurred: the journey was made

A no-show reaches the office as a finished trip with nobody on board, not as a trip that disappears from the day.
