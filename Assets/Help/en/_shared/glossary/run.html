Route

A vehicle, a driver and a span of dates and hours. It is the unit trips are assigned to.

reference

A route is the combination of a vehicle, a driver and a span of dates and hours. The office calls it a run, or just "the route".

What it holds#

  • An assigned vehicle and driver.
  • A garage with its coordinates: where it leaves from and returns to.
  • A date range (from / to) and a time window.
  • A phone login, which the driver's application identifies itself with. It is what makes a driver see their own day and not somebody else's.

Why it matters day to day#

Trips are assigned to routes. A trip with no route is a trip nobody is going to make: it exists in the system, it appears in the lists, and there is no vehicle behind it.

When a notice says a trip "needs a route", that is exactly what is missing.
